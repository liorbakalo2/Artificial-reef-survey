## A. Missing — new items not on the master list

### A1. Two Iberian/Mediterranean multifunctional-reef references, cited by name in the JMSE Introduction — directly contradicts master list row 33
The Introduction's very first substantive sentence about world ASR attempts cites three references together:

> "...attempts to establish multifunctional submerged artificial surf reefs (ASRs) made of Geotubes have been made around the world... [14–16]."

Reference 14 is the Henriquez 2005 Delft thesis (already captured, as the citation for Pratte's Reef). References **15 and 16 are NOT captured anywhere in the master list**, and both name a country/coast in their own titles:

- **[15]** Voorde, M.; Antunes do Carmo, J.; Neves, M. **"Designing a Preliminary Multifunctional Artificial Reef to Protect the Portuguese Coast."** *J. Coast. Res.* 2009, 25, 69–79.
- **[16]** López, I.; Tinoco, H.; Aragonés, L.; García-Barba, J. **"The Multifunctional Artificial Reef and Its Role in the Defence of the Mediterranean Coast."** *Sci. Total Environ.* 2016, 550, 910–923.

This directly contradicts the master list's own closing note in §1 (row 33): *"Sites named in the project brief that do NOT appear anywhere in Lior's 8 notes... and any Spanish/Portuguese/Basque/Brazilian/Japanese/Dutch project. None of these names occur in any of the 8 source notes or the image indexes."* — false for Portugal (ref 15) and for a Mediterranean-coast defense reef ([16] is a Spanish-authored study of "the Mediterranean coast," almost certainly the Valencia/Alicante coast given the authors' affiliation). Per the task's strict rule ("a reef mentioned only in a citation title still counts as mentioned"), both must be added as rows in §1, flagged as citation-only leads (no in-text detail beyond the title), with citations-to-chase = the two references above plus a search for which specific Portuguese/Spanish site each paper covers.

*(Lower-priority, same paragraph: refs [4] Armono 2003 "hollow hemispherical shape artificial reefs" (Canadian Coastal Conf., Kingston, Canada) and [6] Fatimah et al. 2008 "Artificial Mangrove Root System (ArMS)" (COPEDEC, Dubai) are also cited as alternative reef technologies in the same intro paragraph, but neither title names a built project's location — Kingston/Dubai are the conference venues, not project sites — so these are technology-category mentions, not missing "place/year" project rows. Flagging for awareness only, not as a required master-list addition.)*

### A2. A walkable-geotube breakwater project mentioned in the transcript (~01:03:40–01:04:01) — not captured
Immediately after the Queensland clay-cliff sandbag example (which the master list does capture as `queensland-clay-cliff-sandbags`), the same ICM speaker gives one more distinct example that the master list drops:

> "...or you know this is a breakwater niche or breakwater using geo tubes rather than rocks just because the the KPIs and you know the end result they wanted um the users to be able to like walk along it and and and um access them and that's basically it in a nutshell."

This is a separate, named-as-an-example project (unnamed location) using **geotubes instead of rock specifically so the public could walk on/access the structure** — a materials-choice-for-amenity story distinct from every other transcript example. It should be its own row in §1, citations-to-chase = same ICM talk, ~01:03:40–01:04:01, location/name to be identified.

### A3. A Haifa municipal-budget cost benchmark from Lior's own notes — not captured in §3
`geotubes in israel and the england surf reef.txt` (the Hebrew geotube/Boscombe note) contains a budgeting section (§3 of that note) that is summarized in the master list only via the already-known Gold Coast $2.5M and Ashkelon 10.5M NIS benchmarks. It omits a third, Israel-specific benchmark Lior explicitly constructed:

> "...בחרתי בפרוייקטי בניה של סקייטפארקים בעיריות בארץ, בתור הדבר שיכול לדמות... כמה ישקיעו על שיפור הספורט הימי. תקרת התקציב היא 8 מ' ש"ח, בחיפה למשל יש גם אחזקה שנתית של כ-0.5 מ' ש"ח... ראיתי שבשביל תכנון שוברי גלים מדובר על סביבות ה-0.5 מ' ש"ח, מניח שבשביל פרוייקט מסובך... התמחור לתכנון יהיה לפחות כפול."
> (Translation: "I chose municipal skatepark construction projects as the closest proxy for what a city would invest in improving water sports. Budget ceiling ≈8M NIS; Haifa's skatepark also carries ≈0.5M NIS/yr maintenance... I saw that breakwater *design* alone runs around 0.5M NIS, so for a complex project like this the design fee alone would likely be at least double [~1M NIS].")

This is Lior's own economic reasoning for what a Haifa reef proposal might need to budget for (construction ceiling ~8M NIS by analogy to a skatepark, ~0.5M NIS/yr maintenance, and a ~1M NIS standalone design-phase estimate) — it belongs in master-list §3 ("Lior's own proposal") alongside the other economics figures (surfer counts, wave-pool price benchmarks, Gold Coast/Ashkelon cost benchmarks) and is currently absent.

1. **§1, row 33 (closing note)** states that no Portuguese or Spanish/Mediterranean project name occurs in any source. This is contradicted by JMSE references [15] and [16] (see A1 above), which are cited by title directly in the paper's Introduction, one of the two richest primary sources.
