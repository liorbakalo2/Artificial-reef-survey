Intake 2026-10-10 (papers intake agent). Source: papers/Review of artificial reefs ... NSW 2013.pdf (131 PDF pages, text layer OK, no OCR needed).

**Full citation:** Blacka, M.J., Shand, T.D., Carley, J.T. and Mariani, A. (2013). A Review of Artificial Reefs for Coastal Protection in NSW. WRL Technical Report **2012/08** (Final, issued 11 June 2013; WRL project 2012001), UNSW Water Research Laboratory, for the NSW Office of Environment and Heritage. https://doi.org/10.4225/53/58e1dc7368c7a. Licence as recorded in REQUESTS_FOR_LIOR.md: CC BY-NC-ND 3.0 AU.
**Report number:** the PDF title page says Technical Report 2012/08. REQUESTS_FOR_LIOR.md P8 says "No. 1012/08" (typo) - corrected in the row.
**Page convention:** report page = PDF page - 12 (front matter is roman numbered). Below: "p.N" = report page, PDF page in brackets when useful.
**What it is:** an independent engineering review of 32 submerged constructed reefs (SCRs), with summary Tables 4.1-4.4 (p.63-69, PDF 75-81) and detailed Appendix A for only six sites: Cable Station (A.1), Narrowneck (A.2), Mount Maunganui (A.3), Dupont Palm Beach FL (A.4), Midtown Palm Beach FL, Prattes (A.11), plus other US breakwaters. **No Appendix A case study for Boscombe, Kovalam, Opunake or Borth**: they appear only in the tables and text of Sections 2-4. "Palm Beach" in P8 = Palm Beach, **Florida** (not our palm-beach-gold-coast).
**Datums:** table 4.2 "Submergence" and "Water depth" datums are mixed and mostly unstated (Cable: MSL; Narrowneck: AHD; Prattes: MLLW; Borth: at MSL; Opunake: at LAT; Mount Maunganui, Boscombe, Kovalam: not stated). "Offshore distance" = distance to the undisturbed pre-construction shoreline (note 2). "Longshore length / cross-shore width" are the maximum dimensions for V-shaped reefs (note 1). Costs in Table 4.4 are corrected to approximate 2013 AUD (so they will not match nominal costs in the cards).

Card status legend: HAS / LACKS / CONTRADICTS as in the P3 notes.

## General P8 findings for the Haifa section (cite once, not per card)
- 32 SCRs reviewed (29 for protection). About half of protection structures had no significant accretion versus predicted; 55% of submerged breakwaters successful (p.82, Section 4.5 p.73).
- Of 5 MPRs only Kovalam may protect; Narrowneck, Boscombe, Mount Maunganui minor/negligible; Borth unknown (p.70).
- 8 surf reefs built; about half at least partially successful (p.69).
- Vertical displacement 0.5-2 m and localised scour seen at most structures; scour at toes, roundheads and gaps (p.72).
- Cost rates: ASR/MPR $250-550 per m3 (p.71); MPR min $7,000-10,000 per m of coast protected vs submerged breakwaters $1,500-5,500 and rock seawall $5,000-10,000 (p.71-73).
- Authors conclude single-objective coastal-protection structures work more reliably than multi-objective (Exec Summary p.iv).
