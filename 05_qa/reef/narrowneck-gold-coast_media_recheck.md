This was **not** a problem with the source URLs — re-downloading into an isolated subfolder (`nnrecheck/`) with unique filenames reproduced the correct, expected images every time (byte-identical Wikimedia file; ICM/Wix images matching their captions). All classifications below are from the verified re-download, not the corrupted first pass.
