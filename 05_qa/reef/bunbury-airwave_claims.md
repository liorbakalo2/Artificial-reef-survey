## Overall

The dossier's factual backbone (dimensions, cost figures, install date, tear cause, quotes from Bottegal/Osborne/Perron/Brennan, tide data) was accurate and well-corroborated across independent sources — 25 of 33 distinct claim-groups checked out clean on first fetch. The main problems found were citation-accuracy errors, not fabrications: three separate claims (the "lift, peak, hold the swell" design quote, the bathtub-origin story, the "A-frame/left-right breaks/closeouts" claim, and part of the Motivation section) had been tagged to R7, a SurferToday article that does not contain any of that text — all were retraced to their actual source and re-cited. The date on R8 was also wrong ("undated") when the page's own metadata gives 2022-03-22. The most consequential fix is substantive rather than cosmetic: R18, previously the dossier's own top-flagged "unfetched, high-priority follow-up," turned out to be retrievable via archive.org and reveals an entire intermediate chapter (a planned December 2020 second prototype installation, requiring further funding) that was missing from the dossier's timeline between the December 2019 tear and the 2022/2024 Bunbury Mail coverage; combined with newly-checked 2025 Waveco company activity, the dossier's "current status" claim has been substantially upgraded from a flat "unconfirmed/unclear" to a sourced account of what happened in between, while still correctly not claiming the original inflatable Airwave was ever confirmed reinstalled.
