## Summary

This is Lior's own working file, not a literature source. It has two parts. Pages 1–4 are a first-principles field-measurement exercise defining what counts as a "minimum surfable wave" for a candidate Israeli reef site and correlating that with real buoy data from the **Shikmona buoy** (off Haifa, Israel Marine Data Center / isramar.ocean.org.il) for three date windows in July–August 2025, cross-checked against two surf-forecast sources (Surfline/Magicseaweed and an app/site called "4Surfers"). Page 5 is Lior's own sketch/proposal for a cheap, light, **temporary and removable** artificial-reef structure (a low frame or trestle weighted down with ocean rocks) meant to raise the effective sea-floor level so incoming swell breaks predictably over it. There are no named existing global artificial reefs in this file — it is 100% Lior's own data/design content, i.e. primary material for the Israel/Haifa section of the project rather than the "reefs around the world" survey section. Swell/approach directions recorded are tightly clustered at 284–294° (roughly WNW), which is the relevant swell window for a Haifa-area (Shikmona) reef design.

### Definition of "minimum surfable wave" (page 1, "Sea Conditions")
- Measurements ("Measures") were taken only on days meeting these criteria:
  - **Significant Wave Height:** 0.45 m – 0.6 m
  - **Maxima(l) Wave Height:** 0.6 m – 0.8 m
- Forecast cross-checks for the same days:
  - **Surfline (MSW — i.e. Magicseaweed, now merged into Surfline):** 0.6 m – 0.9 m
  - **"4Surfers" (surf forecast source/app):** 0.65 m – 0.8 m, described as "knee high"; period 6 s – 8 s; height range restated as 0.85 m – 0.65 m
- **Swell direction for the surfable window: 285° – 294°** (i.e. WNW, matching the 284–294° chart annotations above).

### Depth measurements at the candidate site (page 1, "Depths Measures")
Two bounding scenarios, each giving three depth bands (measured from a reference "Ocean Floor" datum, front view of the wave):

| Depth band | Low Bound (SWH 0.45 m, MWH 0.6 m) | High Bound (SWH 0.6 m, MWH 0.8 m) |
|---|---|---|
| No Breaking* (top of band) | 1.45 m | 1.7 m |
| Wave Breaking Depth | 1.3 m | 1.5 m |
| Min Depth** (bottom of band, at ocean floor) | 1.15 m | 1.35 m |

- Bands are cumulative/stacked (No Breaking sits above Wave Breaking Depth, which sits above Min Depth, which sits above the Ocean Floor) — read as a vertical section from the sea floor upward.
- Depth measurements were taken **~1 second after the wave broke**, at a marked reference position illustrated by an arrow on a wave-crest sketch ("Note: Depth Measurement Position").
- **Terminology notes (verbatim):**
  - `*` "The max height of 'No Breaking' represent min break depth for mega sets." — i.e. the upper bound of the No-Breaking band is the minimum depth at which even the largest ("mega") sets will not break.
  - `**` "'Min Depth' represent the breaking depth of the smallest part of the wave that is still possible to surf on (Meaning – smallest shoulder/intersection)."

### Length measurement (page 1, "Length Measures", side view)
- Taken under the same sea conditions as the depth measurements.
- **Accuracy level: ± 0.5 m.**
- Horizontal distance labeled **"2 m Until Break"** — the length from the point where "Wave Breaking Depth" begins to where the wave actually breaks, shown on a side-view diagram with the same Min Depth / Wave Breaking Depth / Ocean Floor stack as the front view.

### Hebrew annotations (pages 3 and 4, identical yellow-highlighted note, appears twice)
- Hebrew (verbatim): "צהוב = גבולי – היה גליש רק בסטים הגבוהים שהגיעו אחת לרבע שעה"
- English translation: "Yellow = borderline/marginal — it was surfable/glidable only on the high(er) sets that arrived once every quarter hour."
- This annotates the yellow-shaded time windows on the Shikmona buoy charts on pages 3 and 4, distinguishing them from the green (apparently reliably surfable) and red (apparently not surfable) shaded windows.

### Lior's proposed structure — depth/engineering intent (page 5)
- Diagram: "Temporary Construction" / front view, two side-by-side columns over "Ocean Floor":
  - Left column = the natural/no-structure depth profile: "No Breaking" (top) over "Wave Breaking Depth" over "Min Depth" (bottom, at floor) — i.e. the same three-band stack defined on page 1.
  - Right column = the same three-band stack but with a drawn structure (a low frame/trestle on short legs, with piles of rocks at its base on both sides) placed on the ocean floor, rising up through the "Min Depth" band to about the "Wave Breaking Depth" band.
- Caption/label: **"Light Construction — Uses cheap & light materials alongside ocean rocks for stability to ensure fast deployment and removal."**
- Read together with page 1, the implied engineering intent is: place a low, removable, rock-ballasted frame on the sea floor so that its crest sits near the natural "Wave Breaking Depth" line, effectively shallowing the site enough to trigger breaking at the desired depth without permanently altering the sea floor (hence "temporary" and fast to deploy/remove).
