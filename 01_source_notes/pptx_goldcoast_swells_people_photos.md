## Summary

This deck is a working file of the project owner ("Lior," who signs an outreach email on slide 17) and mixes three distinct kinds of material, exactly as the task brief expected: (1) field/forecast wave measurements at a Haifa-area spot, used to define what counts as a "surfable" wave (slides 1–8); (2) a design sketch for a cheap, temporary, removable reef structure (slide 9), plus a page for cheap-vendor links for a physical/foam demo mock-up (slide 10 wave-pool clippings, slide 11 vendor note); and (3) a large bundle of Israel-specific coastal-engineering and advocacy material — buoy data, bathymetry sources, Netanya/Ashdod/Haifa coastal-protection news, key contacts at Israel's coastal-cliff-protection company, a satellite comparison of famous artificial reefs (Narrowneck, Cables, Mount Reef, Pratte's Reef), a screenshot from a Gold Coast/Australian coastal-management company's webinar explicitly framing "The Gold Coast Example – Artificial Reef" as a multi-purpose sediment/resilience/ecology tool, and a draft cold-outreach email to the Israeli NGO Zalul (צלול) pitching an artificial-reef pilot in Israel and explicitly citing Israel's small tidal range as an advantage for reef stability. Despite the "Gold Coast" wording in the filename, the deck contains only **one** direct Gold Coast/Narrowneck visual (a third-party webinar screenshot, image29) — it is not a dedicated photo essay of Narrowneck; most of the deck's weight is Israeli field data, contacts, and advocacy material. - Sea-state criteria used to define a "surfable" wave in the study period: Significant Wave Height (SWH) **0.45–0.6 m**, Maxima Wave Height (MWH) **0.6–0.8 m**.
- Forecast cross-checks for the same days: Surfline (MSW) **0.6–0.9 m**; "4Surfers" forecast **0.65–0.8 m** ("knee high"), period **6–8 s**, height range restated as **0.85–0.65 m**.
- Swell direction during measurement window: **285–294°**.
- A depth table (slide 2) gives, for "Low Bound" (SWH 0.45 m, MWH 0.6 m) and "High Bound" (SWH 0.6 m, MWH 0.8 m) conditions, three depth figures each: **1.3 m, 1.15 m, 1.45 m** (Low Bound) and **1.5 m, 1.35 m, 1.7 m** (High Bound), associated with the column headers "Min Depth," "Wave Breaking Depth," and "No Breaking." *Caveat: the raw XML text extraction does not preserve the original table's row/column layout with certainty, so the exact pairing of each number to its column could be off — the six numbers and the two bound labels themselves are exactly as stated in the file.*
