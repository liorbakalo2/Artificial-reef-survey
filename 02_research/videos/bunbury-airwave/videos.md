## Why only one usable video

The Airwave was only ever partially installed once (Dec 2019) before tearing;
it was never successfully completed, and public reporting found no further
on-site video after that. Waveco's own promotional videos predate the Bunbury
deal (2018 Kickstarter scale-model tests) or are a now-dead live-cam stream.
Honesty over quantity: rather than padding the list with unrelated or
unverifiable videos, this index keeps only the one confirmed, on-site clip.
