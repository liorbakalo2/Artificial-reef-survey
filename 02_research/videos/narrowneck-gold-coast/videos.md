## 1. "NARROWNECK REEF" — Griffith Centre for Coastal Management (2015, 4:03) — PRIMARY
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oUfLGStUKPs

Interview documentary with the reef's design engineer: why it was built (Northern Gold Coast Beach Protection Strategy, 1999), why a submerged geotextile-bag reef beat groynes/beach dewatering, how the split-hull dredge barge placed the sandbags, and ~10 years of monitoring/marine-growth results. Transcript: `oUfLGStUKPs.txt` (deduped from English captions).

Key quotes:
- 00:19 — "The [Narrowneck] Reef is a geotextile bag reef and its primary objective was Coastal protection."
- 01:00 — "...the one that came out best was a submerged artificial Reef without the added bonuses of Surfing and Diving — it was clearly the best solution."
- 01:33 — "We've constructed a structure that you can't see but it's constantly reducing the sand transport and the storm cut so we have a wider Beach, a more stable Beach."
- 01:57 — "McQuade won the contract for dropping the sandbags — he had bought in a split Hull Hopper barge that he developed specifically for doing this type of work."
- 02:52 — "The difference between a geotextile structure and a concrete or rock structure is the type of growth we get — 10 m of water we're getting Kelps, we're getting Turtles, and I've dived with dolphins."
- 03:32 — "The data collected over the last 10 years... clearly show that it's been a highly successful device or structure for maintaining the health of the beach."
