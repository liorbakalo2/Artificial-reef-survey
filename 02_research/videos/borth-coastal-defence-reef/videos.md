## Transcripts
None obtained. All four are silent/no-narration amateur clips (`yt-dlp --list-subs` confirmed no automatic captions and no manual subtitles for the three construction videos checked). A fifth candidate, "Snorkelling on Borth Reefs" (Alan Cole, 2016, 2:08, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ToT4lZ9yW0A), does have English auto-captions and could show the completed reef underwater, but (a) its subtitle download repeatedly failed with YouTube HTTP 429 rate-limit errors this session, and (b) the title's plural "Reefs" makes it uncertain whether it shows the constructed multi-purpose reef specifically or Borth's natural boulder-reef coastline — so it was not included above. Flagged here as a lead for a later agent, not used.
