## Notes for the writer / verifier

- **Best new "built and clearly successful" case**: Southern Ocean Surf Reef, Albany WA (row 7) — very recent (opened July 2025), well documented, unambiguous "success judged on wave quality alone" framing that's rare among ASRs.
- **Best new "resolves an existing master-list gap" find**: Leirosa, Portugal (row 12) is very likely the actual site behind the bare `portuguese-coast-reef-voorde-2009` citation already in `00_master_list.md` §1 — same authors' research thread (Voorde/Antunes do Carmo/Neves, LNEC), same "multifunctional reef" framing. Not 100% confirmed as the *same* structure the JMSE paper's ref [15] describes — a later agent should pull the actual Voorde et al. 2009 paper and check its site name against "Leirosa."
- **Two separate Portugal sites** found (Leirosa/Figueira da Foz and São Pedro do Estoril/Cascais) — do not conflate them into one dossier card.
- **Two separate Perth, WA sites** found: Cable Station Reef (built 1999, row 5) and the Scarborough Beach proposal (proposed only, row 10) — both in Perth but different suburbs/projects.
- **Kovalam, Opunake, Chevron Reef, and Boscombe** are now four confirmed "built then substantially failed/removed" world cases beyond the already-known Pratte's Reef — useful cluster for a "why most ASRs fail" section, alongside the Wikipedia "Multi-purpose reef" line that fewer than ten such attempts have been made worldwide.
- **Two disambiguation traps flagged** (rows 21, 23) so a later agent doesn't burn time on them again.
