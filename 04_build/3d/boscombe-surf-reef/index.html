Survey datum
Datum switch moves the whole surveyed seabed + reef up by 1.40 m against the water (see Methods A3); the CCO beach profiles are referenced to ODN independently and do NOT move (the beach then no longer joins the survey: evidence for chart datum). The Navionics shoal lines do NOT move: with the switch on, the survey no longer matches them (evidence for chart datum).
