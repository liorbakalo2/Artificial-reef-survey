## Step 2a - tracing img1 and img5 (2026-10-05) - FIRST TRACE, SUPERSEDED by Step 6 (verification run: it wrongly included ~49 m2 of dashed 'optional' bags; all numbers in this step and in Step 3 are those of the first trace, 483.9 m2, and are NOT the final values)
- Viewed img1 zoomed (grid 5-10, scale 4-12): the drawing uses SOLID outlines for built bags (legend "BAG COUNT: 110") and DASHED outlines for the 30 "OPTIONAL BAGS" (not built). Dashed bags lie on the OUTER (offshore/up-wave) side: two outer diagonal rows on each arm near the spine, and the leftmost column of the spine (partly). The earlier draft trace in shape.json (vertices at x=88, 115,98 ...) followed the OUTER DASHED boundary, i.e. it wrongly included optional bags -> discarded.
- Method used instead (both images): binarise at gray>200, label white regions, keep those fully enclosed (area 30-600 px, not touching the border, not in scale bar/legend); a bag with a solid outline encloses its interior as a closed white region, a dashed bag leaks into the exterior and is NOT captured. Enclosed-region count: img1 = 98 regions (+ a few bags whose outline is broken by an annotation line), img5 = 96 (printed count is 110, so ~12-14 solid bags are missed as separate regions, which the next steps fill). Dilated the union of regions by 1 px (outline centre-line), closed gaps along the two 45-degree arm directions with a 25-px line structuring element (+2-px disk), filled holes, took the outer contour (OpenCV findContours), simplified 0.7 px (shapely). Result: img1 66 vertices, 12858 px2; img5 75 vertices, 16650 px2.
- Visual check (overlay rendered on 4x upsampled image, three crops): outline follows the outer edge of the solid-outlined bags along both arms, the spine and both elbows; dashed optional bags are excluded.
- Sensitivity (img5, thr 190/200/215, dilation 1/2 px): envelope area 14.7k-17.9k px2 -> +-10% depending on threshold (low threshold misses broken-outline bags) and +-4% for 1 px of dilation. Threshold 200, r=1 chosen because the overlay shows every visible solid bag inside the outline.
- SCALE img1: bar labelled 15', 30', 60', 90' (the 0 tick is cut off by the image edge), tick x positions from run-length scan of rows 329-336: 20.5, 43.5, 91.5, 138 (px). Least-squares over these 4 ticks: 1.5712 px/ft (=5.155 px/m, 0.1940 m/px), 0 ft at x=-3.2, residuals <=0.45 px. (The draft's 5.06 px/m used a spurious 0-ft tick at x=0.)
