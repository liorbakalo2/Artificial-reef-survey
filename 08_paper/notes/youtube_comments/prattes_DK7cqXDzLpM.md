# Pratte's Reef: surfer comments on YouTube video DK7cqXDzLpM

Source: comments on https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DK7cqXDzLpM (posted about 7-8 months before 2026-10-19).
Copied by Lior on 2026-10-19 and pasted to the session. These are quoted excerpts. Evidence grade D (anecdotal, user comments):
use them only as surfer testimony, never as measurement.

| user | excerpt (verbatim) | point |
|---|---|---|
| @johnlong1100 (worked at the adjacent power plant 1975-82, 1988-2002) | "The sand bag reef never produced a surfable wave. I was there 365 days a year and never saw one." "It was positioned where it would only ever have a chance from a wind swell. The south swells wouldn't reach it because of Catalina Island blocking the swell ... The northwest swells didn't make it either" | no wave; swell shadowing by Catalina and the land mass |
| @spencerparker4691 | "my buddies and I surfed Prattes reef on a couple of occasions when it actually broke. It was really tide sensitive and wave height sensitive. It was never going to handle legitimate overhead swell ... The wave came from deep water, hit the shallow reef quickly, then instantly went into deep water again. The wave would suck up fast, but before it could actually barrel, it would mush out and disappear. It was barely surfable." "The reef was shaped like a V instead of a triangle" | rare, barely surfable; too short/small (supports insight a) |
| @krokus1972 (geologist) | "it did nothing. A major letdown." "The approach of shore at that location is too steep. The swells had no time to build before they encountered the tiny reef. Resulting in a tiny close out wave." | site choice, too small |
| @camerongieda (helped with survey work, USC 1990s) | "We surfed over it a few times, but it was never big enough to hold any decent sized surf. The only thing it was good for was attracting lobster" | too small; some ecology benefit |
| @kirbyharness9696 | "The reef never produced anything from my knowledge." | no wave |
| @BananaPistachio | "Never saw it break other than once when a big swell came through. Even then it only modestly gave it any shape better than the closeouts." | no wave |
| @TheTactical001 | "we use to surf it it was so so, the use of sand bags was a joke you need hard structure like boulders" | material criticism |
| @feedbackart | "I saw them pull the bags out. The polymer bags were still intact." | bags were intact at removal (B: material survived; failure was surf, not durability) |
| @RonMiks-k6h | "the V-shape reef, use of sand bags and poor selection for reef placement ... Bummed nothing ever broke." | design and siting |

Rating (Lior, 2026-10-19): A (surf) = 1.
Note for B: one witness says the bags were intact when removed, so the failure was the wave, not the material.
