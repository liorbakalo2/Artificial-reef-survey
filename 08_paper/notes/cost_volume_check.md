# Check of Table 1 costs and volumes (2026-10-20)

Scope: `08_paper/data/reefs_table.csv` (cost_local, cost_usd, cost_usd_2024, volume_m3), `03_survey.md` Table 1, reef cards, `05_qa/papers_intake/`, 3D HANDOFFs. Nothing was applied; corrections are listed at the end.
Web caveat: WebFetch and curl to the news and council sites failed (DNS or 429), so web values come from search-result summaries only. Those rows are marked "search only". No page was read in full.

## Conversion method (checked)
- `cost_usd` = local amount / World Bank annual-average official FX of the cost year (nominal). `cost_usd_2024` = nominal USD x US CPI(2024)/CPI(year), with 2025 treated as 2024 (`fx_worldbank.json`, `data/README.md`). Arithmetic re-computed for all 12 rows: correct.
- The Table 1 column "Cost (USD M, 2024)" is inflation-adjusted with US CPI only. The price year is the construction or completion year; for multi-year spends (Opunake, Boscombe, Borth) it is a single year. The caption does not state this and should.
- Scope is NOT uniform: reef only (Boscombe, Cables approx., Palm Beach), reef + studies (Narrowneck, Cables, MM), whole defence scheme (Borth). The caption says only "volume as stated"; it says nothing about cost scope.

## Does Lior's suspicion hold?
- Albany: yes, 11.75 M is a committed-funds figure; the final reported cost is about AUD 13 M.
- Narrowneck: the volume is real as a literature sand-fill number (70,000 m3, Mead & Borrero 2011) but is not the volume of the reef as seen. The mound is about 13,500 m3 in our 3D. The cost is the build only, so it is probably low for the life-cycle (2.8 M by 2007 + about 2 M renewal).
- "Others probably cost more": confirmed for Borth (the 28 M is the whole scheme, so it is too high for the reef) and for Opunake (probably 2.0-2.1 M, not 1.7 M). Boscombe (3.2 M reef + 0.7 M repairs), Narrowneck (see above) and Albany are higher in a life-cycle reading. Palm Beach, Pratte's, MM, Kovalam, Cables, Burkitts agree with their sources.
