## Downloaded

### [22] Yucatan, Mexico: González Leija et al. (2014), ICCE poster 25
- File: `05_qa/papers_intake/bar_drimer_cited/ref22_gonzalez_leija_2014_text.md` (PDF: `ref22_gonzalez_leija_2014.pdf`), 1 page, text layer read in full.
- Key facts:
  1. Geotube tubes were laid as semi-submerged breakwaters 10 to 30 m off the shore at several Yucatan sites in 2005-2007, where scarps reached about 1 m (p. 1, Introduction).
  2. Some sites recovered (positive profile and shoreline advance), but at others a basic negative freeboard (Fb about -0.3 m at MSL) led to a downdrift shadow effect; the authors say the design needs a very gentle shoreward slope, otherwise the structure acts as a groin (p. 1, Fig. 2).
  3. Wave-flume tests (UNAM Instituto de Ingenieria) found that the transmission coefficient Kt is governed by one parameter, the ratio of submergence depth to incident wave height (p. 1, Experimental Results).
  4. Concluding remarks: geotextile structures act much like impermeable concrete ones; if crowned well below mean water level, waves steepen and break more violently; the maximum recommended freeboard should not exceed twice the dominant wave height (p. 1, Concluding remarks 1-3).
  5. Relative crown width helps dissipation, but for real dimensions the protection level can be given by submergence depth over incident wave height (p. 1, Concluding remarks 4).
- Relation to the survey: this is the laboratory study cited by BD23. The field project is a separate matter (see appendix D.2, which cites Alvarez et al. 2006 and the 2007 Geotextiles and Geomembranes paper; neither is in the BD23 list).

### [21] Young-Jin beach, Korea: Oh & Shin (2006), Coastal Engineering 53, 879-895 (ref not downloaded)
- Appendix D.1 gives: submerged double-lined geotube detached breakwaters; shoreline advance of 2.4 to 7.6 m in 12 months, then local re-erosion (level READ, from the 8ICG paper and the Coastal Eng. text). Re-read today: not possible (Elsevier paywall; no open copy found).

### [23] Sigandu Beach, Indonesia: Sulaiman et al. (2015), Procedia Eng. 116, 510-519 (ref not downloaded)
- Abstract summary seen in a search excerpt on 2026-10-19 (not the PDF): erosion attributed to the Batang port jetty; geotube low-crested breakwaters installed from September 2014; about a month later salients and tombolos had formed behind the structures (abstract level).

### [8] Al Aqah, UAE: Chien et al. (2014), Int. J. Environ. Sci. Dev. 5, 506-509 (ref not downloaded)
- Appendix D.3 gives: geotube groins and a submerged breakwater for nourishment after cyclone Gonu; tube circumference 8.6-17.2 m (vendor level, ACE Geosynthetics case study). The journal host (ijesd.org) was unreachable today.

### [24] Ashkelon, Israel: Gouaud (2016), design report (ref not downloaded, not online)
- Appendix D.8 and `08_paper/notes/israel.md`: 48 tubes in five clusters at the cliff foot; several tubes torn after the first winter (State Comptroller 2020, p. 71, read). The Gouaud report itself was not found.

### [7] Living Breakwaters, Staten Island: Marrone et al. (2019), Coastal Structures Conf., Hanover (ref not downloaded)
- Search today returned other Living Breakwaters ICCE papers (Baker et al., ICCE physical model studies) but not this paper. Appendix D.5 gives the project context (eight partly submerged armour-stone and concrete breakwaters, USD 111 M, completion announced Sept 2024; press level).

### [15] Portuguese coast multifunctional reef: ten Voorde, Antunes do Carmo & Neves (2009), J. Coast. Res. 25(1), 69-79 (open copy exists, see Open items; not yet in intake)
- A search excerpt (not the PDF) said the design is delta-shaped in its upper part and only works on sea slopes gentler than 1:50, and that the reef sits more than 1.5 times the natural surf-zone width from the undisturbed shoreline (abstract level, 2026-10-19).

### [16] Babilonia, Guardamar (Mediterranean) MFAR: Lopez et al. (2016), Sci. Total Environ. 550, 910-923 (ref not downloaded)
- Appendix D.7: a design study; the abstract states a surfable wave 60 % of the time for low to intermediate surfers (abstract level via University of Alicante record, not confirmed in full text).
