- Cost basis: Palm Beach 18.2 M and Narrowneck 2.3 M are the build figures reported by the source. Narrowneck's life-cycle figure (2.8 M to about 2007, plus about 2 M renewal) is not in any single primary source read here.
- Borth: the £13 M is the whole first-phase scheme (BBC via Coflein). It must not be used as a reef-only cost. This matches the earlier pass.
- Albany: the 11.75 M is committed funds; the 13 M is the reported final cost. The City project profile (PDF) was fetched but is binary and was not read; the City FAQ figure (11.5 M) is in the earlier pass.
