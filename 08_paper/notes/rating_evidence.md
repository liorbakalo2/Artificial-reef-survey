# Rating evidence: surf (A), safety and longevity (B), other effects (C), 13 reefs

Prepared 2026-10-19 from the repo's own notes and cards. Only this file was written. No other file was edited; no git.

**Sources read:** `08_paper/data/reefs.json` (outcome fields: `surf_outcome`, `surfable_days_per_year`, `durability`,
`sediment_shoreline_outcome`); `08_paper/notes/surf_outcomes.md`, `materials.md`, `sediment.md`; `08_paper/analysis/FINDINGS.md`
(section 6), `SEABED_SEDIMENT.md` (sections 1-2); reef cards `02_research/reefs/<slug>.md|json`, grepped for injury, safety and hazard
terms (Albany read in full).

**Scale used for the suggested scores:** 1 = very poor, 2 = poor, 3 = mixed or neutral (includes "no measurable effect"),
4 = good, 5 = very good. `?` = unknown (no usable evidence). Scores are suggestions for Lior to decide. Confidence is in the reason column.

**Citation keys** (full citations are in the notes' source sections): BLA13 = Blacka et al. 2013 (NSW review, printed page; PDF = page + 12);
MB11 = Mead & Borrero 2011; RND16 = Rendle 2016 thesis (printed page; PDF = page + 3); BN03 = Borrero & Nelsen 2003; PRE22 = Prenzler et al.
2022 (ICCE abstract); COR23 = Corbett et al. 2023; card = reef card in `02_research/reefs/`. Grade: A measured, B engineering report or thesis
(designers are interested), C journalism or surfer media, D forum or single anecdote.

## 3. Points for Lior

1. **Surf day counts are not comparable across sources.** The 142 (Cables) and 146 (Albany, derived) figures are weak; Albany's 40% is unsourced. Keep A scores on shape and reports, not day counts.
2. **Narrowneck crest:** BLA13 says -1.0 to -1.5 m AHD; the card says Swellnet gives 1 m. This changes the A story. The notes have not resolved it.
3. **Albany safety is the only B item where injuries are the main evidence.** The sources are a forum (D) and trade press (C); no injury counts. Only the 2026 rock repositioning is a formal action.
4. **Mount Maunganui C is negative (rips), not neutral.** This is a council statement reported by the NZ Herald (C), not a monitoring result.
5. **Mexico material conflict:** surf_outcomes.md says "boulders", materials.md says "not stated". Not resolved.
6. **Cables material conflict:** granite (BLA13) vs limestone (MB11). Affects B only if the B score is revised.
7. Scores for Borth and Palm Beach C are provisional because the only positive signals are not attributable (SEABED_SEDIMENT, sediment.md).
