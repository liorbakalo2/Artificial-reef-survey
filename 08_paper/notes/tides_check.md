# Tide-range check for fig1 and reefs.json (2026-10-19)

Question (Lior): are the tide ranges in `08_paper/figures/fig1_plan_and_profiles.png` wrong (Bunbury, Cables, Albany, maybe Narrowneck look too small)?

Short answer: Narrowneck is right. Bunbury and Albany are too small, and so is the Cables band. The value for Opunake and Burkitts is also wrong or unsupported. Several rows are only partly checked (see the status column and section 4).

## 1. How the paper uses tide ranges

- Figure band (`08_paper/analysis/figures_survey.py`, `tide_band()`, lines 98-103): the band is MLWS-MHWS if both are in the profile JSON; otherwise MLLW-MHHW; otherwise LWt-HWt (Cables, +/-0.4 m). The caption (line 510) calls it the "spring tide range" and says "MLLW-MHHW at diurnal and mixed sites".
- So at Bunbury, Albany and Prattes the band is the great diurnal range (MHHW-MLLW), not the spring range. At Cables it is a symmetric +/-0.4 m taken from Blacka et al. (2013) Table 4.3, whose definition is not given.
- `reefs.json` fields `tide_spring_range_m` and `tide_mean_range_m` are the values the text and tables use. Profile planes come from `08_paper/data/profiles/<slug>.json` (`tidal_planes_m_above_msl`).

## 3. Which values must change

1. **Bunbury** (`reefs.json` tide_spring_range_m 0.4, tide_mean_range_m 0.25; profile band MLLW-MHHW): recompute from the full WA DoT 2026 Bunbury predictions. The 0.4 m is not supported by the predicted daily ranges (for example 0.93 m on 1 Jan 2026).
2. **Albany** (`reefs.json` tide_spring_range_m 0.6; profile band MLLW-MHHW 0.6): recompute the same way. Predicted extremes span about 1.3 m.
3. **Cables** (`reefs.json` tide_mean_range_m 0.8; figure band +/-0.4 m labelled "spring"): the band is not a spring range. Get the official Fremantle planes (BoM / Australian tide tables) and redraw the band from them. The paper's MHWS-MLWS should replace the +/-0.4 m.
4. **Opunake** (`reefs.json` tide_mean_range_m ">3.0"): this is a spring-type value. Use Port Taranaki spring 3.16 and mean 2.35 (LINZ 2026-27), with the reference port stated.
5. **Burkitts** (`reefs.json` tide_mean_range_m 2.5): replace with the MSQ Burnett Heads planes already held in `_no_3d_model.json` (spring 2.33, mean 1.74), after the row is verified.
6. **Prattes** (`reefs.json` tide_spring_range_m 1.65): value is correct but labelled "spring". Relabel as the great diurnal range (MHHW-MLLW). No value change.
7. **Narrowneck and Palm Beach**: no change. The band in the figure (1.31 m) is correct. It looks small only because the scale is 1 to 1.5 m; the figure's vertical scale does not show that the band is wrong.
8. **Mount Maunganui, Borth**: no change.
9. **Boscombe**: no change needed on the evidence available, but confirm against the Admiralty Tide Tables before the paper is final.
10. **Figure caption** (`figures_survey.py` line 510): the caption says "spring tide range". At diurnal and mixed sites the band is MHHW-MLLW, so the caption should say so. Not edited here (per instructions).

## 4. Method, confidence and unknowns

- The WA DoT / BoM PDFs (2024 and 2026) give daily high and low heights referenced to LAT. Extremes were extracted by script (`python3 -I` over `pdftotext -layout` output). 3 of 4 quarterly blocks per port were parsed, so the extremes are lower bounds of the true extremes; the spreads are indicative, not MHWS-MLWS. A proper spring/neap or MHHW/MLLW computation needs a column-aware parser for the day rows; that was not done here.
- Network: the proxy blocks some government hosts (MSQ, LINZ, BoM, gov.wales return 403 by policy; they were not retried). The MSQ and LINZ values above come from search-engine extracts that quote the official documents, not from a direct read of the PDFs. They agree with the paper's stored planes, which is independent support.
- Admiralty Tide Tables / TotalTide (UK, Boscombe and Aberystwyth), Vizhinjam (India) and Manzanillo (SEMAR) were not reached. Their rows are UNCLEAR.
- Datum: the WA predictions are referenced to LAT; the paper's Bunbury and Albany planes use GHD and WA-AHD relations. Converting between them is a source of error and should be stated in the paper.
- Sources read: `08_paper/data/reefs.json`, `08_paper/data/profiles/*.json`, `08_paper/analysis/figures_survey.py`. No other file was edited. Nothing was run through git.
