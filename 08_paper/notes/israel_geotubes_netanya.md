# Israel geotubes: was a Netanya geotube torn? (checked 2026-10-15)

Author: sub-agent, Round 1 (paper team). Scope: the torn geotubes Lior remembers (Netanya, winter about 2019, about a year after deployment), plus every other Israeli geotube or geotextile project found. Working files: `/tmp/claude-0/paper/geotube/`.

## 1. Bottom line

- **Netanya is NOT confirmed.** No source found (Lior's files, the local dossiers, my web searches, or the official reports) shows a geotube built, laid or torn in Netanya. Netanya's cliff works were rock breakwaters (6 northern ones built, 2021-2023; southern phase later), with sand feeding. Geotubes were only a permitted option in the national cliff master plan.
- **The torn geotubes Lior describes were at Ashkelon North (Ashkelon cliff foot), 2018-2019.** The 7 Feb 2019 Haaretz report and the 2020 State Comptroller report describe Ashkelon North tubes damaged or torn after laying in the first winter. The 2024 comptroller report says the Ashkelon North geotube protection "collapsed" (PDF p.29) without a date. The timing Lior remembers (winter, about a year after laying) matches Ashkelon. The location he remembers (Netanya) does not.
- **Lior's own text** (`Initial info from Lior/geotubes in israel and the england surf reef.txt`) describes the Ashkelon cliff project ("מול מצוק אשקלון"). It does not mention Netanya. The source notes (`01_source_notes/lior_notes_geotubes_and_boscombe.md` line 61, `01_source_notes/jmse2023_bar_drimer_paper.md` line 131) say so explicitly.
- **Two numbers in Lior's note are not supported by the official sources:** "15 of 48 destroyed before completion" (no count of destroyed tubes in any opened source), and "10 dunam, 4 m high" (no dimensions in any opened source). His depth-step hypothesis (4-5 m to 2-3 m) is also not in any source; it is his opinion and is flagged as such.
- **The existing Ashkelon dossier (`02_research/israel/ashkelon-geotubes-2018.md`) has an unsupported claim:** "17 destroyed by storms, 2 by boats" (Dec 2018-Jan 2019). I could not find that count in any source, and the official reports say only "several" tubes tore. The Haaretz 2019 article also says the last storm "was not so severe" to explain the damage. See section 6.

## 9. Suggested handling for the paper

1. Cite S1 and S2 (PDF pages above) for the Ashkelon geotube facts, not Lior's notes. Lior's figures (48 tubes, NIS 10.5M) can be used as "reported by the client" with his note as the source.
2. Describe the Ashkelon North tubes as "damaged and torn within the first winter after laying" (S2 PDF p.71). Do not give a destroyed count or storm dates until a source is found.
3. State plainly in the paper that no geotube was built at Netanya, and that Netanya's protection is rock breakwaters (S2 PDF p.71; S3 PDF p.48; S4).
4. Use the 2024 comptroller's failure-mode list (S3 PDF p.9: fabric brittleness, poor filling, propeller, anchor and diver-knife damage) as the official basis for the geotube fragility argument, which fits Lior's Boscombe and Ashkelon hypotheses.
5. Open items: obtain the Calcalist article (dispute, contract value, date); the State Comptroller summary report on the Ashkelon pilot (not yet submitted per S3 PDF p.55); a Hebrew source for the Ashkelon tube dimensions and the beach name.
