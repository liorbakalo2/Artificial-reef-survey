# Appendix B. Three-dimensional reconstructions

Nine of the 13 reefs had a sourced plan, crest level and seabed depth, and were reconstructed as three-dimensional models of the reef, the surrounding seabed and the local water levels (Section 2.3). The models show the as-built state; later changes such as settlement, renewal or removal are described in Section 3 and Appendix A, not drawn. All heights are relative to mean sea level through the datum chain of each reef (Appendix C, note 1), and only the tidal planes of the nearest standard port are used. The depth profiles of Figure 1, the computations of Sections 4 and 5 and the volumes marked "model" in Table 2 are taken from these models. Figure B1 shows the default version of each; the inputs, sources, assumptions and confidence of every model are listed per reef in Appendix A.
