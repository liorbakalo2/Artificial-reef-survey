# Appendix C. Reef data table

This appendix reproduces the master data table of the review in condensed form: 13 surf reefs, ordered by the year in which construction started, with 38 data fields per reef. The reef numbers 1 to 13 are those of the sections A.1 to A.13 of Appendix A. The table is compiled from the project dataset (files `reefs.json` and `reefs_table.csv`, held with the project files and available from the author). In the dataset every field is a record with a value, a unit, a status, the full citation and the place where the value was read (page, table, figure or file); only the status is reproduced here, and the principal sources and the methods behind each reef are given in Appendix A. The sources of the outcome and durability entries are cited in Table C4.

**Key to the status markers.** A marker follows each entry and applies to all numbers in the cell.

| Marker | Meaning |
|:-----|:--------------------------------------------------------------|
| ^s^ | **sourced**: a number or statement read in a cited source |
| ^d^ | **derived**: computed in this review from sourced inputs, or from a traced plan shape or a reconstructed 3D model; the method is in Appendix A |
| ^a^ | **assumed**: an assumption of this review, flagged in Appendix A (A-numbers) |
| ? | **unknown**: not found in any source examined; no value is given |

The outcome classes (works, works rarely, does not work, mixed or disputed, unknown) are this review's reading of the cited sources, marked ^s^ because the underlying statements are sourced; they are not measurements. Units: depths and heights in metres, depths positive downward; areas in m^2^; volumes in m^3^.

## Notes to Tables C1-C5

1. **Datums.** Each 3D model is referenced to mean sea level (z = 0 at MSL). Conversions applied: Narrowneck and Palm Beach z~MSL~ = z~AHD~ - 0.12 m (Maritime Safety Queensland, Gold Coast Seaway; the rule used in the papers would shift this by 0.24 m); Cables, Bunbury and Albany: Australian Height Datum (AHD) taken as MSL (Cables low water datum is 0.716 m below AHD; Albany LAT is 0.74 m below AHD); Mount Maunganui z~MSL~ = z~CD~ - 1.13 m (Land Information New Zealand, Tauranga, present MSL); Boscombe z~MSL~ = z~ACD~ - 1.40 m (survey zero assumed to be chart datum); Borth z~MSL~ = z~ODN~ - 0.31 m; Pratte's z~MSL~ = z~MLLW~ - 0.849 m. Opunake and Kovalam have no usable datum.
2. **Ramp and slope.** The ramp is the horizontal toe-to-crest distance on the shore-normal transect through the shallowest crest cell; the crest edge is the seaward-most cell within 0.25 m of that column's maximum (the dataset gives the sensitivity to 0.10 and 0.50 m). Ramp and slope are derived from the reconstructed 3D models for Narrowneck, Cables, Albany, Palm Beach and Borth (median over the columns of the long diagonal mound), from the idealised loft of the 3D model for Boscombe (the 1:3 flank is an assumption), by hand from the 2013 digital elevation model for Mount Maunganui, and from the spherical-cap stand-in for Bunbury. **Pratte's ramp and slope are assumed (1:1 bag face).** Where a sourced crest depth exists (Cables, Palm Beach, Albany) the depth gap uses it. Only Narrowneck, Cables, Albany, Palm Beach, Borth and Boscombe have a ramp from audited geometry; relations should not be fitted through the whole set without the status markers. Table C2 keeps the master-table definition (shallowest crest point); the surf analysis of Section 4 (Table 2) reads Albany, Cables and Borth on a surf cut through the ridden part of the reef (Appendix A, item 4; Figure E1 in Appendix E), so its crest depth, gap, ramp and slope differ for these three reefs (Albany 1.97 m, 5.40 m, 99 m, 1:19.8; Cables 1.92 m, 4.59 m, 45 m, 1:23.7; Borth crest 0.47 m above MSL, 4.77 m, 40 m, 1:9.5).
3. **Plan areas are definitions, not always the reef.** Narrowneck: visible container field on the aerial of 8 August 2020, a lower bound. Cables: original design plan. Pratte's: Phase I design layout. Mount Maunganui: the -2.0 m CD contour of the 2013 multibeam survey (the 2008 toe outline is 1,424 m^2^). Opunake: design bounding rectangle 99 x 30 m, not the traced reef; the bag layout is not published, and the along-shore and cross-shore values in Table C2 are the published design size with unknown orientation. Boscombe: outline traced on the image of 28 September 2011. Kovalam: photo trace at an assumed camera height of 60 m (550-1,700 m^2^ for 30-80 m), low confidence. Borth: **area is the visible rock edge on the photograph of 17 September 2024; the volume (30,119 m^3^) belongs to the laser-survey edge of 2022 (8,675 m^2^)**, the default of the 3D model. Palm Beach: toe outline. Albany: Bluecoast design outline (the dark rock patch visible in 2026 is 10,918 m^2^). Bunbury: circle of 12 m diameter traced on a drone photograph. Narrowneck volume (70,000 m^3^) is the sand fill stated by the designers; the 3D mound envelope is a different quantity.
4. **Distance offshore** is measured from different shoreline definitions: the MSL waterline of the image used (Narrowneck 8 August 2020, Palm Beach 1 December 2025, Pratte's), the wet/dry-sand edge (Mount Maunganui, Albany beach-face line), the back-of-beach defence line (Borth), the pre-reef shoreline (Kovalam, as stated in the NSW review) and a survey surf line (Boscombe). Differences below 30 m are noise.
5. **Costs.** Costs are in nominal local currency as stated by the source, for what each source calls the reef; they may include studies. The Borth figure (GBP 13 M) is for the whole Phase 1 defence scheme and is not a reef cost. USD = local amount divided by the World Bank annual-average official exchange rate of the price year; USD (2024) = nominal USD multiplied by the US consumer price index of 2024 divided by that of the price year (2025 uses the 2024 index) [@worldbank2026]. Both are derived and inherit the uncertainty of the price year.
6. **Years.** "Built" is the start of construction and "completed" the end of the main phase. Reefs with soft structures that failed or were removed are listed with their original construction years.
7. **Coordinates** are the centres of the traced outlines (derived); Bunbury is an estimated centre (assumed); Pratte's is the centre of the survey window of @borrero2003 (position +-150 m); Opunake is the centre of the consent area; Burkitts is the platform centre; Xala is undisclosed.
8. **Not tabulated** (available in the dataset): seabed depth at the crest, mean tide range definitions, design-wave statements, swell direction, arm angle to the swell, and the alternative values (other published or derived values for the same quantity, each with its own status and source).
9. **Cautions.** Typical swell (Hs, Tp, direction) is unknown for most reefs because the design reports of the original designers were not read in full; the Haifa wave data are not part of this table. Cost conversions assume the price year given. Values of Mount Maunganui (assumed bed), Bunbury (stand-in surface) and Pratte's (assumed slope) are weak and are marked.
