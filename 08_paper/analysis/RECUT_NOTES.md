## 1. What changed in the method (for Methods / Appendix A wording)

- `extract_profiles.py` keeps the automatic rule (primary cut `crest_line` = constant model x through the shallowest crest
  cell) for six reefs, and adds `CUT_OVERRIDES`: a per-reef SURF CUT, a line through the part of the reef where the wave
  breaks and is ridden, perpendicular to the local shoreline, sampled every 1 m by the same bilinear interpolation on the
  model grids (now also along an oblique line). Each override has a written reason ("Lior 2026-10-19: ...") in the code and
  in each profile JSON (`lines.crest_line.definition` / `.override`); the old automatic line stays as
  `lines.crest_line_auto` (reference only, not used downstream). `profiles/SUMMARY.md` has a new section 1a listing them.
- Every downstream script (surf, coastal, stability, summary, Figure 1, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9) uses `crest_line`, so they all now
  use the surf cut for these three reefs. Volumes, footprints and hypsometry do not depend on the cut and are unchanged.
- Suggested sentence: "For Albany, Cables and Borth the automatic crest line did not cross the part of the reef where the
  wave breaks, or was not shore-normal; for these reefs the profile is taken along a cut through the surfed part of the
  reef, perpendicular to the local shoreline (Appendix A)."

## 4. Albany: the "Y" issue (Lior: "Albany shows Y > 1.6 in Fig. 4 yet works")

- In the old Figure 4, Albany's point (ramp 22 m, gap 6.15 m, mean face 1:4.0) lay in the band left of the 1:12 line, the
  band labelled "collapsing (Y < 1.6)": mean-face vortex ratio Y = 0.065 X + 0.821 = 1.08, i.e. "beyond extreme, likely to
  collapse, unsurfable" (Mead 2000 p.121). On that line 26 % of on-reef breaks were surging/collapsing and 47 % had Y < 1.6.
  A reef that works sat among the collapsing reefs.
- Cause: the cut. The automatic line ran through the shallowest cell, the -1.0 m AHD take-off plateau at the offshore end,
  where the model's outer flank drops 6 m in 22 m. The ride is along the -2 m ridge, which the wave reaches over the long
  seaward body of the reef.
- On the surf cut (ridge middle) the ramp is 99 m, mean 1:19.8, gap 5.40 m: Y of the mean face = 2.11 ("very high / high",
  the Palm Beach class), median xi_b 0.56 (plunging), no surging cases, median Y at the break 1.75 ("extreme": hollow,
  for advanced surfers, consistent with the design's 60 deg peel and Hutt ratings 2-7). RESOLVED: Albany now plots next to
  Palm Beach, the other reef that works.
- Caveats: the Albany 3D surface is a fitted design rule (LOW confidence); the ridge surface is nearly flat (-2.0 to -2.4 m)
  for about 80 m and the steep face (max 1:3.5) is near the toe, so the mean slope hides a steep outer step; the swell at
  the reef is probably oblique (refraction in King George Sound), which a shore-normal 1-D cut ignores.
