**Figure B1.** Three-dimensional reconstructions of the nine modelled reefs (default version of each model; screenshots of the interactive viewer, panels cropped away). These are reconstructions, not photographs or surveys: each combines a traced or designed outline, a surveyed or inferred seabed, crest levels from design or survey documents and the local tidal planes, and its confidence differs by reef; the inputs, sources and assumptions of every model are listed per reef in Appendix A. Tiles are not at a common scale (use the scale bars and labels inside each tile); views are oblique except (c) and (d), which are plan views because the oblique preview was cut by the viewer panels; vertical scale is true except (i) with 2x exaggeration. The symbol before each name gives the surf outcome as in Figure 1 (circle works, triangle works rarely, diamond mixed or disputed, cross does not work, small hollow square unknown); the name is written in the reef's colour used in all figures.
