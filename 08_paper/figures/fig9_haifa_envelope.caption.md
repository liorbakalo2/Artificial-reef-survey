**Figure 9.** Design envelope for a surf reef at Haifa on a synthetic 1:50 bed (1:70 gives the same result; no Haifa bathymetry is available), as a function of crest depth below MSL and seaward ramp slope (crest 10 m wide, back slope 1:3). Cell colour is the fraction of the time the break is surfable under the Israeli tide (spring range 0.40 m; the same for ramps from 1:12 to 1:30 and falling with crest depth), the bar in each cell the share of waves (H0 = 0.6 to 1.5 m, T = 6 to 8 s, at MSL) that are too hollow, advanced or expert, spilling or gentle, or do not break on the reef (classes from ξ_b and Y after Battjes, 1974, and Mead and Black, 2001), and the contours the reef volume per 100 m of length (10,000, 15,000, 25,000 and 40,000 m³). The heavy box is the recommended envelope (crest 0.6 to 1.0 m, ramp 1:16 to 1:20, 15,000 to 25,000 m³ per 100 m, rock armour Dn50 of about 0.7 to 0.85 m); the arrow shows 1 m of settlement or seasonal bed rise (crest 0.8 to 1.8 m), the dashed lines the crest depths implied by field breaking depths observed at Haifa (Hs 0.45 to 0.60 m; Bakalo, 2025, unpublished field notes), and the symbols the built reefs by crest depth and mean seaward slope on their surf cut (Palm Beach, Cables, Albany on its -2 m ridge, Narrowneck second crest line; Boscombe, 1:3.1, is off the scale), coloured and labelled per reef, with the outcome as symbol shape. The model is one-dimensional linear shoaling with γ = 0.78 and no refraction or focusing, so the envelope is a first screening and needs a Haifa wave climate and a two-dimensional wave model or flume test.
