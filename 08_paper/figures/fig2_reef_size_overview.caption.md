**Figure 2.** Size overview of the 13 surf-reef projects (in order of construction): plan length along the shore and footprint area of the traced outline, volume as stated by the sources (rock or sand fill, not one common quantity), crest depth below MSL (negative: crest above MSL, Borth) and depth gap between the seabed at the seaward toe and the crest. Colour and name identify the reef; symbol shape gives the surf outcome (legend below the panels; unknown = small hollow square). Light-filled symbols mark assumed, low-confidence or not directly comparable values and 'unknown' means that no source checked gives the value; the four reefs without a traced shape (Opunake, Kovalam, Burkitts, Xala) therefore have no length or footprint. Values are those of the master table `08_paper/data/reefs.json` (sources and status of every value in Appendix C; reading of the outlines and 3D models in Appendix A; main sources include Blacka et al., 2013; Mead and Borrero, 2011; Rendle, 2016; Corbett et al., 2023); lengths, areas, crest depths and gaps are derived from traced outlines and 3D models, volumes are sourced except Borth and Bunbury (derived).
