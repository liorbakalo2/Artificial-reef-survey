**Figure 6.** Reef volume against outcome for the eleven projects with a known outcome (filled symbols: volume stated in the sources; open symbols joined by a line: volume of the reconstructed 3D model, which differs most at Narrowneck, 70,000 against 13,496 m³). Colour and label identify the reef, symbol shape the outcome (circle works, triangle works rarely, diamond mixed or disputed, cross does not work), repeating the vertical axis. The three reefs that do not work are 4,900 m³ or smaller (Bunbury 105, Pratte's 1,350, Opunake 4,900 m³) and both built reefs that work (Palm Beach 25,000 and Albany 34,000 m³) are 25,000 m³ or larger; Burkitts (*) works at 300 m³ because it is a natural reef reshaped by moving about 300 m³ of rock, so volume matters when the reef has to create the whole relief. The Spearman rank correlation with the ordinal outcome (works 3, works rarely or mixed 2, does not work 1) is ρ = 0.72 (permutation p = 0.021, n = 10) for the stated volume of the built reefs, 0.43 (p = 0.21, n = 11) when Burkitts is included and 0.93 (p = 0.005, n = 8) for the model volume, and 0.87 (p = 0.003, n = 10) when the exposed mound of Narrowneck (13,495 m³) and the built volume of Opunake (about 3,400 m³) replace the sand fill and the design volume (Table 1, notes); the sample is small, the outcome is a judgement and volume is collinear with material (the large reefs are recent rock reefs) and budget, so the association is descriptive only (Blacka et al., 2013; Mead and Borrero, 2011; Corbett et al., 2023 and the sources listed in Appendix C).
