**Figure 4.** Depth gap Δh (toe bed depth minus crest depth) against ramp length L (from the seaward edge of the crest to the seaward toe) for the nine modelled reefs, read on the surf cut of each reef (Figure 1), on logarithmic axes. On these axes every line of constant mean gradient Δh/L = 1/X is a straight diagonal of the same direction, so the breaker-type zones, which depend on the gradient only, appear as parallel diagonal bands: steeper (upper left) means a more intense, plunging to collapsing break, gentler (lower right) a spilling break. The grey bands are the breaking-intensity classes of Mead and Black (2001), from the vortex ratio Y = 0.065 X + 0.821 (Mead, 2000, Eq. 6.5; class limits Y = 1.6, 1.9, 2.5 and 3.1 at X = 12.0, 16.6, 25.8 and 35.1, i.e. slopes 1:12 to 1:35); Y < 1.6 is "beyond extreme", likely to collapse and unsurfable (Mead, 2000, p. 121). The dashed and dotted lines are the Iribarren limits ξ_b = 0.4 (spilling/plunging) and 2.0 (plunging/surging) (Battjes, 1974; Lewis et al., 2015) for a breaking height of 1 m with T = 10 s and with T = 7 s, representative of Israeli seas. Colour and label identify the reef; symbol shape gives the outcome (circle works, triangle works rarely, diamond mixed or disputed, cross does not work, small hollow square unknown). Hollow "ghost" symbols joined by a thin line show another cut of the same reef: Narrowneck's main crest line (16 m, 2.08 m) against its south-arm line (37 m, 3.92 m), Pratte's crest line (1 m, a tip artefact) against the apex line (5 m), and Albany's former automatic cut through the -1 m take-off plateau (22 m, 6.15 m, in the collapsing band) against the surf cut on the long -2 m ridge (99 m, 5.40 m, very high intensity). The gradient Δh/L includes the natural bed slope between crest and toe, so it is steeper than the reef-face slope at some reefs (Cables 1:9.8 here against 1:23.7 on the face); geometry is idealised for six of the nine reefs. Spearman correlation with the ordinal outcome (n = 8, Borth unknown): depth gap ρ = 0.93 (permutation p = 0.005), ramp length 0.93 (0.005), crest depth on the cut 0.39 (0.36).
