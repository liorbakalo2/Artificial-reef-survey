**Figure 4.** Depth gap Δh (toe bed depth minus crest depth) against ramp length L (from the seaward edge of the crest to the seaward toe) for the nine modelled reefs, read on the surf cut of each reef (Figure 1), on logarithmic axes. On these axes every line of constant mean gradient Δh/L = 1/X is a straight diagonal of the same direction, so the breaker-type zones, which depend on the gradient only, appear as parallel diagonal bands: steeper (upper left) means a more intense, plunging to collapsing break, gentler (lower right) a spilling break. The grey bands are the breaking-intensity classes of Mead and Black (2001), from the vortex ratio Y = 0.065 X + 0.821 (Mead, 2000, Eq. 6.5; class limits Y = 1.6, 1.9, 2.5 and 3.1 at X = 12.0, 16.6, 25.8 and 35.1, i.e. slopes 1:12 to 1:35); Y < 1.6 is "beyond extreme", likely to collapse and unsurfable (Mead, 2000, p. 121). The dashed and dotted lines are the Iribarren limits ξ_b = 0.4 (spilling/plunging) and 2.0 (plunging/surging) (Battjes, 1974; Lewis et al., 2015) for a breaking height of 1 m with T = 10 s and with T = 7 s, representative of Israeli seas. Colour and label identify the reef; symbol shape gives the outcome (circle works, triangle works rarely, diamond mixed or disputed, cross does not work, small hollow square unknown). Each reef is plotted once, for its surf cut, the profile line of Figure 1; where the lines and arms of every reef run is shown in Figure E1 (Appendix E). Only Albany carries a second, hollow symbol, joined to the main one by a thin line, because its two cuts have a different physical meaning: the main cut (filled circle: 99 m, 5.40 m, very high intensity) runs along the long -2 m ridge where the left-hander is ridden (ride section), and the second cut (hollow: 22 m, 6.15 m, in the collapsing band) crosses the short, shallow -1 m offshore plateau where the surfer takes off, which is steep because it is the focusing peak of the reef and not the surfed face. Second cuts that had no such meaning (Narrowneck's north-arm cut, which clips the tip of the arm, and Pratte's cut through the tip of the apex) were dropped. The gradient Δh/L includes the natural bed slope between crest and toe, so it is steeper than the reef-face slope at some reefs (Cables 1:9.8 here against 1:23.7 on the face); geometry is idealised for six of the nine reefs. Spearman correlation with the ordinal outcome (n = 8, Borth unknown): depth gap ρ = 0.93 (permutation p = 0.005), ramp length 0.93 (0.005), crest depth on the cut 0.39 (0.36).
