**Figure 1.** Plan shapes (upper part of each row) and cross-shore depth profiles (lower part) of the nine surf reefs whose outline is known, in order of construction; Opunake, Kovalam, Burkitts and Xala (Mexico) are not drawn because no outline could be traced. All plans share one metric scale in x and y (1 cm = 70 m at print width; 100 m bar at lower right), are viewed looking seaward (unmirrored), and each outline is set at its distance from the MSL shoreline (thick line, y = 0) with arbitrary alongshore slots; the hatched shape is Borth's southern oval, a shore-parallel breakwater rather than a surf reef, and Bunbury is the 12 m Airwave bladder, torn on installation. Each profile is the section of the 3D reconstruction marked by the dashed line (the section whose crest depth best matches the master table), drawn at the plan's horizontal scale (window shifted per reef) with a vertical exaggeration of about 21:1; elevations are relative to MSL, the blue band is the spring tide range (MLWS-MHWS, or MLLW-MHHW at diurnal and mixed sites; 0.8 m total range at Cables, Blacka et al., 2013), and C, T and G are the crest depth, the seabed depth at the seaward toe and the depth gap (T - C) read from the plotted section, within 0.35 m of the master-table values.

Build and data notes (not part of the caption)
- Files: `fig1_plan_and_profiles` (both rows, 17 x 22.6 cm); the same rows separately as `fig1_plan_and_profiles_rowA` and `_rowB` (17 x 11.3 cm each) if a full page is too tall. Code: `08_paper/analysis/figures_survey.py` (function `fig1`), palette in `08_paper/analysis/style.py`.
- Plan outlines: DEFAULT outline of each `07_scale/shapes/<slug>/shape.json` (Cables: original design plan of Lyons 1992 as reproduced in Blacka et al. 2013 Fig. A.2, fitted to the Nov 1999 soundings; Narrowneck: container field on the Esri image of 8 Aug 2020; Pratte's: Skelly Engineering design drawing of the Phase I layout; Mount Maunganui: -2.0 m CD contour of the 18 Jul 2013 multibeam survey; Boscombe: outline traced on Esri World Imagery Wayback imagery (frame origin = surf line of 28 Sep 2011); Borth: Welsh Government LiDAR edge of 19 Mar 2022; Palm Beach: toe outline traced on the Bluecoast/Nearmap aerial of the completed reef; Bunbury: 12 m circle traced from the 2019 drone photo, orientation arbitrary; Albany: Bluecoast final-design outline). Per-reef sources and confidence: Appendix A, `shape.json` and `08_paper/data/profiles/<slug>.json`.
- Frame: every `shape.json` frame is 'x alongshore, y offshore' with +y = +x turned 90 deg clockwise, so x is plotted as -x to give an unmirrored view looking seaward; the Cables frame has the other handedness (x north, y west) and its outline is rotated into the fitted 0 m AHD shoreline (bearing 7.8 deg; 254 m east of the anchor, `geo` block of its `shape.json`), so the axes there are exactly shore-parallel and shore-normal. Distance offshore = model y minus the model y at which the section's seabed crosses MSL (`y_msl_crossing_m`: Borth 90.6 m, Boscombe -8.0 m, Narrowneck -5.0 m, Albany -5.3 m, Bunbury 3.2 m; Cables: fitted shoreline, 3.1 m; Prattes, Mount Maunganui and Palm Beach 0). Shoreline definitions differ by reef (MSL waterline of an aerial image, wet/dry sand edge, back-of-beach line at Borth; `data/README.md`); differences below 30 m are noise. DERIVED.
- Sections: `08_paper/data/profiles/<slug>.json`, lines: Cables crest_line, Narrowneck second_crest_line (south arm), Pratte's centreline (apex), Mount Maunganui second_crest_line, Boscombe crest_line, Borth centreline (northern reef), Palm Beach crest_line, Bunbury crest_line, Albany crest_line. Chosen as the line whose crest depth is closest to `crest_depth_msl_m` of `reefs.json`. The Cables section follows the design-frame y axis, about 17.5 deg off the shore normal (distances along the line, not shore-normal). Seabed under the reefs is interpolated except at Cables (1998 pre-construction survey); crest height and ramp slopes inherit the 3D-model assumptions (profiles/SUMMARY.md section 6). Datums: each model uses z = 0 at MSL with the conversions listed in `data/README.md` (Boscombe, Albany and Prattes depend on stated assumptions).
- Tide band planes from the profile JSONs (`tidal_planes_m_above_msl`); Cables +-0.4 m is half of the 0.8 m range of Blacka et al. 2013 Table 4.3, whose definition is not given in the source.
- Annotated C, T, G versus master table (max difference): C 0.27 m (Borth, Pratte's), T 0.32 m (Palm Beach), G 0.32 m (Palm Beach).
