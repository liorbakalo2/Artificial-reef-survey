The same H0 and T were chosen for both panels (FIGURE_SPECS F3), so the Boscombe panel is not its typical sea.
- Water surface: Airy dispersion and shoaling from `wavelib.py` (`wavenumber`, `shoaling_coefficient`), phase integrated from the break point seaward; wave height capped at γ d. Landward of the break (replaces the earlier flat still-water level): `breaker_shapes` / `bore_train` / `mean_level` in `figures_wave.py`: trough 0.38 H_b below the mean level just in front of the breaking crest (crest 0.62 H_b above it), the back of the previous broken wave rising linearly over L_b = T sqrt(g h_b) = 41.8 m to a bore crest 0.20 H_b above the mean, a steep bore front to the next trough (-0.20 H_b, both decaying by 0.8 per wave); mean level -0.05 H_b at the break (gamma H_b / 16, Longuet-Higgins and Stewart 1964) rising linearly to +0.25 H_b at the model MSL shoreline (coefficient UNVERIFIED, order of magnitude, notes/fig3_theory.md s1). Time step: t0 + 1 s, crest advanced by c_b = sqrt(g h_b) = 4.18 m. Plunging lip about 0.15 H_b thick at the root (Peregrine 1983, typical range 0.1-0.2 H). Overview x range extended to 55 m landward of the crest edge so that the first bore front is in view. Breaker class for the detail drawing: plunging when ξ_b is between 0.4 and 2.0 or when it is plunging by the table and Y >= 1.6 (Palm Beach); collapsing front otherwise (Boscombe, Y < 1.6). ASSUMPTION (graphic only): back of the wave a half cosine of 9 m, lip and foam drawn by hand.
