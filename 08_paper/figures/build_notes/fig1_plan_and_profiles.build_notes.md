Build and data notes (not part of the caption)
- Files: `fig1_plan_and_profiles` (both rows, 17 x 23.1 cm incl. the outcome-symbol legend strip); the same rows separately as `fig1_plan_and_profiles_rowA` and `_rowB` (17 x 11.3 cm each) if a full page is too tall. Code: `08_paper/analysis/figures_survey.py` (function `fig1`), palette in `08_paper/analysis/style.py`.
- Plan outlines: DEFAULT outline of each `07_scale/shapes/<slug>/shape.json` (Cables: original design plan of Lyons 1992 as reproduced in Blacka et al. 2013 Fig. A.2, fitted to the Nov 1999 soundings; Narrowneck: container field on the Esri image of 8 Aug 2020; Pratte's: Skelly Engineering design drawing of the Phase I layout; Mount Maunganui: -2.0 m CD contour of the 18 Jul 2013 multibeam survey; Boscombe: outline traced on Esri World Imagery Wayback imagery (frame origin = surf line of 28 Sep 2011); Borth: Welsh Government LiDAR edge of 19 Mar 2022; Palm Beach: toe outline traced on the Bluecoast/Nearmap aerial of the completed reef; Bunbury: 12 m circle traced from the 2019 drone photo, orientation arbitrary; Albany: Bluecoast final-design outline). Per-reef sources and confidence: Appendix A, `shape.json` and `08_paper/data/profiles/<slug>.json`.
- Frame: every `shape.json` frame is 'x alongshore, y offshore' with +y = +x turned 90 deg clockwise, so x is plotted as -x to give an unmirrored view looking seaward; the Cables frame has the other handedness (x north, y west) and its outline is rotated into the fitted 0 m AHD shoreline (bearing 7.8 deg; 254 m east of the anchor, `geo` block of its `shape.json`), so the axes there are exactly shore-parallel and shore-normal. Distance offshore = model y minus the model y at which the section's seabed crosses MSL (`y_msl_crossing_m`: Borth 86.9 m, Boscombe -8.0 m, Narrowneck -5.0 m, Albany -5.3 m, Bunbury 3.2 m; Cables: fitted shoreline met by the oblique cut at line coordinate 19.6 m; Prattes, Mount Maunganui and Palm Beach 0). Shoreline definitions differ by reef (MSL waterline of an aerial image, wet/dry sand edge, back-of-beach line at Borth; `data/README.md`); differences below 30 m are noise. DERIVED.
- Sections: `08_paper/data/profiles/<slug>.json`, lines: Cables crest_line (surf cut), Narrowneck second_crest_line (south arm), Pratte's centreline (apex), Mount Maunganui second_crest_line, Boscombe crest_line, Borth crest_line (surf cut, northern reef), Palm Beach crest_line, Bunbury crest_line, Albany crest_line (surf cut). For Albany, Cables and Borth `crest_line` is a per-reef SURF CUT set in `extract_profiles.py` (`CUT_OVERRIDES`; review of 2026-10-19): Albany x = 17 m (model frame, shore-normal) through the middle of the -2 m AHD ridge (ridge cells y 68-168 m), replacing the automatic line x = 48 m through the -1.0 m take-off plateau; Cables through the footprint centroid (x -5.0, y 274.8 m) at offshore bearing 277.8 deg T, perpendicular to the fitted 0 m AHD shoreline (bearing 7.8 deg T; the model seabed's mean down-slope direction over the reef is 17.9 deg from the model y axis, the same within 0.4 deg), replacing the constant-x line x = -18 m; Borth x = -43.5 m, 10 m south of the former plotted centreline (x = -33.5 m). The automatic line is kept in each JSON as `lines.crest_line_auto`. For the other reefs the line is the one whose crest depth is closest to `crest_depth_msl_m` of `reefs.json`. The Cables section is sampled along the oblique line by bilinear interpolation of the model grids; its distances are along the line, which is shore-normal. Seabed under the reefs is interpolated except at Cables (1998 pre-construction survey); crest height and ramp slopes inherit the 3D-model assumptions (profiles/SUMMARY.md section 6). Datums: each model uses z = 0 at MSL with the conversions listed in `data/README.md` (Boscombe, Albany and Prattes depend on stated assumptions).
- Tide band planes from the profile JSONs (`tidal_planes_m_above_msl`); the Cables, Bunbury and Albany bands (`ANN_LO`/`ANN_HI`) are the predicted annual range of the WA Department of Transport / BoM *Tide Predictions by Location WA 2026* (heights above LAT; Fremantle +0.30 to +1.44 m, Bunbury +0.10 to +1.30 m, Albany +0.09 to +1.47 m), centred on MSL; three of four quarterly blocks were parsed, so they are lower bounds. They replace the earlier Cables +-0.4 m (Blacka et al. 2013 Table 4.3, definition not given) and the Bunbury/Albany MHHW-MLLW bands (0.4 and 0.6 m). Pratte's band is the NOAA great diurnal range (GT 1.654 m). Verification: `08_paper/notes/tides_check.md`.
- Annotated C, T, G versus master table (difference): Albany C 0.97 m, G 0.93 m and Cables C 0.37 m, G 0.37 m (surf cuts, see above); otherwise at most C 0.28 m (Borth), T 0.32 m (Palm Beach), G 0.32 m (Palm Beach).
- Header symbols: shape = surf outcome class from `reefs.json` `surf_outcome` (`style.OUTCOME_MARKER`; unknown = small hollow square), colour = reef (`style.COLORS`), name written in the reef colour.
