**Figure E1.** Plan-view outlines of the nine reefs with a profile cut, each with the main profile cut (dashed black; Figure 1), the reef arm used for the peel angle φ0 (solid black), and, for Albany, the second cut kept in Figure 4 (dotted red). Seaward is up; north is not up. All panels share one baseline scale, that of the Albany panel (1×; 50 m scale bar): the horizontal window is 347 m wide and the vertical window runs from 28 m behind the shoreline to 347 m offshore, and every panel shows these ranges divided by its zoom factor. Panels at a different zoom are labelled in the top right (Pratte's 3×, Mount Maunganui 3×, Bunbury 5×; scale bar as marked); all other panels are 1×. The shoreline (y = 0) is at the same height in every panel and zoom is about the shoreline. Where a reef lies beyond the window it is shown after a y-axis break (marked //, omitted range stated): Narrowneck (y = 103 to 185 m omitted), Mount Maunganui (21 to 268 m) and Palm Beach (111 to 193 m); the two strips share one scale. Panels are centred on the reef cut. Red φ0 = assumed value; reef colours as in the main figures.
