**Figure E1.** Plan-view outlines of the nine reefs with a profile cut, each with the main profile cut (dashed black; Figure 1), the reef arm used for the peel angle φ0 (solid black), and, for Albany, the second cut kept in Figure 4 (dotted red). Seaward is up; north is not up. All panels have equal aspect (1 m in x = 1 m in y on paper) and share one baseline scale, that of the Albany panel (1×; 50 m scale bar): the horizontal window is 347 m wide and the vertical window runs from 28 m behind the shoreline to 385 m offshore, and every panel shows these ranges divided by its zoom factor. Only two panels are zoomed, both labelled in the top right: Pratte's 3× (20 m scale bar) and Bunbury 5× (10 m scale bar); all other panels, including Narrowneck, Mount Maunganui and Palm Beach, are 1×. The shoreline (y = 0) is at the same height in every panel and zoom is about the shoreline. No panel has an axis break. Panels are centred on the reef cut. Red φ0 = assumed value; reef colours as in the main figures.
