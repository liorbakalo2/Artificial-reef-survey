# Artificial surf reefs — world survey and Haifa proposal

## Goal

Build an interactive HTML page surveying artificial wave/surf reefs built around the world — year, place, cost, who financed them, motivation, design vs. as-built differences, outcome (did they work, why or why not), and real reviews/articles found online — alongside a dedicated section on sediment-transfer interventions in Israel (geo-tubes, Tel-Aviv breakwaters, Haifa Port sand bypassing, Netanya/Herzliya efforts), closing with conclusions on the best way to build an artificial wave reef in Haifa (given Israel's small tidal range advantage) covering location, target wave size, sand preservation, and cost. Every claim is backed with a reference/link to its source.
