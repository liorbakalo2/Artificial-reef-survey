# Kovalam Reef (Lighthouse Beach) — footprint provenance

Status: **verified**, 2026-09-25 (adversarial re-check; see Verification section below). Confidence: **low** (no as-built survey or engineering drawing found; all dimensions below are estimates of varying rigor).

## Summary

- **Shape:** a **chevron / echelon fan** of individual ~30 m geotextile-tube units radiating from a focus near the rocky headland ("Eve's/Lighthouse Point") — **not** a smooth curved crescent or delta wedge.
- **Length × width (bbox):** ≈ 88 m alongshore × 42 m cross-shore (schematic reconstruction; low confidence).
- **Area:** ≈ 1,080 m² (footprint of the tubes themselves, not a filled solid).
- **Crest depth:** not found in any source (ours or Gemini's cited sources).
- **Distance offshore:** not found; our own visual impression from a construction-phase aerial photo suggests a near-shore siting (~35–75 m), but this is not confirmed.
- **Confidence:** low on exact numbers; medium on the shape *typology* (chevron/fan vs. crescent) — resting primarily on one clear photographic source (S3) with a weaker second source (S2), both disagreeing with Gemini's unsourced crescent (see Verification, 2026-09-25, for the correction to how strongly S2 supports this).

## Polygon construction

Coordinate convention: origin at the shoreline point nearest the reef centroid (approximated at the base of the rocky headland immediately updrift of the reef); **x = alongshore**, positive toward the southeast (from the headland toward the main Lighthouse Beach hotel frontage — this sense is read off the ASR Ltd aerial photo's own orientation, since no compass bearing for the coast was found sourced); **y = offshore**, positive seaward; shoreline approximated as y = 0.

Because no as-built survey exists, the polygon is an explicit **schematic reconstruction**, built as follows:
1. Three straight "tube centrelines" were laid out end-to-end in a staggered ("staircase") chain, each segment ≈ 30 m long (matching the sourced bag-unit length), turning at each joint to produce the stepped/zigzag character seen in both S2 and S3.
2. Each centreline segment was buffered by ±6 m (12 m total tube width, an unsourced analogy — see Open Issues) using `shapely`, with flat (butt) caps and mitred joins.
3. The three buffered tube polygons were unioned into a single polygon with `shapely.ops.unary_union`.
4. The result was verified: `is_valid = True`, `is_simple = True` (non-self-intersecting), first vertex equals last vertex (closed), area = 1,083.5 m², bounding box = 87.9 m (x-span) × 41.6 m (y-span), y-range 34.3–75.9 m from the shoreline baseline. *All of the above independently re-verified 2026-09-25 (see Verification section): `is_valid=True`, `is_simple=True`, area=1083.3 m², bbox 87.89×41.61 m, y-range 34.3–75.9 m — all confirmed. The one number that did **not** check out was `max_dimension_m` (see below).*

This polygon should be read as **illustrative of the reef's likely typology and rough scale**, not as a georeferenced as-built outline — no source provides the latter.

## Open issues

- No engineering drawing, GPS as-built survey, or dimensioned figure for the Kovalam reef was found in this pass (ours or Gemini's) — every footprint number here (ours and Gemini's) is an estimate of varying rigor.
- Gemini's two cited sources for crest depth ("Kerala Harbour Engineering Dept Project Report 2011," "Thomas et al. 2012") could not be located and may be fabricated or badly mis-cited — flagged, not resolved.
- Mead & Borrero (2011) "Multi-Purpose Reefs – A Decade of Application" (S4) is very likely the real technical source behind Raised Water Research's own uncredited "Mead and Borrero (2011)" line, and may contain actual Kovalam dimension/performance data, but sits behind a ResearchGate wall and an eCoast email-gated download; not accessed this pass (would have required submitting an email to a third-party form, out of scope for this pass).
- The 12 m tube/crest-width assumption used in our area estimate is not sourced for Kovalam specifically — borrowed by analogy from other ASR Ltd geobag reefs (and coincidentally close to Gemini's own equally-unsourced 12 m assumption).
- The compass bearing of the shoreline/alongshore direction and of the incident swell was not confirmed for this exact site in any source read this pass.
- The "Kovalam-Outline.jpg" hand-traced line could represent either the structure's actual crest outline or simply the visible foam/break-line pattern at one moment of one swell event — no source labels which, so its use here as shape evidence carries residual uncertainty.
- A satellite-imagery check (Google Maps, re-visit of an already-catalogued image type) found no visible remnant of the structure — consistent with, but not additional confirmation of, its reported degraded/submerged state.
- Re-verification (2026-09-25) also opened three more images already hosted on the already-cited R1 page but not previously catalogued — `Kovalam-Geomat.jpg` and `Kovalam-Reef-Underwater1/2.jpg` — plus Gemini's own three cited photos. None contributed any new dimension or changed any number (see Images section, items 5–6, for details); the Geomat photo's exact role is left unresolved.
