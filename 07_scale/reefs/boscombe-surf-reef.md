# Boscombe Surf Reef — footprint provenance

Status: **verified 2026-09-25** (adversarial re-check of the 2026-09-25 research pass; see "Verification 2026-09-25" section at the end). Validated: JSON parses, polygon closed and non-self-intersecting (shapely: valid=True, simple=True, area=10,861.6 m²).

## Summary

Boscombe Surf Reef was a single elongated, asymmetric angled submerged bank ("ramp") of ~55 large sand-filled geotextile containers, not a multi-armed chevron/delta shape. Best estimate: **~187.5 m long (its own long axis) x ~58 m wide**, footprint area **≈10,000 m² (≈1 hectare / 2.5 acres)**, sited **≈220 m offshore** of the seawall, with a designed crest that sat **~0.5 m *above* the low-spring-tide (LAT) datum** after sand settlement — i.e. it was built to nearly (or actually) breach the surface at low spring tides, not to stay submerged. Confidence: **medium overall, but LOW specifically on the 187.5 m / 58 m / ~247° shape numbers** — the area (~1 ha), volume and offshore-distance-range figures are solidly text-sourced from two-plus independent real sources and confirmed on re-check. The detailed long-axis length/width/bearing/"photo cross-check area" are our own pixel measurements from an already-verified aerial photo, cross-checked against the pier's known length as a scale bar; a 2026-09-25 adversarial re-measurement (automated, threshold-based) found these figures far more sensitive to methodology than the originally-claimed ±20–30% — plausible ranges of roughly ±60 m on length, ±30 m on width and ±60% on the photo-cross-check area — so they are now flagged as rough, order-of-magnitude estimates rather than semi-precise measurements. See "Verification 2026-09-25" below.

## Images/figures relied on

1. **`Boscombe-Arial-1024x683.jpg`** (raisedwaterresearch.com, credited to Bournemouth Echo; already in the card's `images/web/boscombe-surf-reef.json`). Downloaded to scratchpad, viewed, and pixel-measured, then deleted. Shows the reef as a single dark, roughly parallelogram-shaped patch in the water, well offshore of Boscombe Pier, with faint parallel internal stripes (the individual container tubes). **What was measured**: (a) the four corners of the dark patch; (b) Boscombe Pier's own base-to-tip pixel length (164.3 px). Using S1's real-world pier length (180 m), this gives a scale of **1.096 m/px**. Applying that scale to the reef corners gives a long-axis (ramp) length of ~187.5 m, a perpendicular width of ~58 m, and a centroid distance offshore of ~208–210 m — this last figure lands inside the 210–225 m range given by three separate text sources (R1, R4, R7). **[RE-VERIFIED 2026-09-25]** Re-downloaded the same photo and independently re-measured it by automated pixel-darkness thresholding rather than eyeballing corners: (a) the pier's own pixel length reproduced closely (~162 px vs. the original 164.3 px, ~1.5% apart) — this is a genuine, solid cross-check of the ~1.1 m/px scale bar; but (b) the reef patch's own major/minor-axis extent turned out to be highly sensitive to exactly where the "edge" of the water-blurred patch is drawn — across 5 reasonable threshold choices, the implied long-axis length ranged ~110–190 m, the width ~20–92 m, and the implied area ~2,200–17,300 m². 187.5 m/58 m/10,861.6 m² sit near the upper end of these ranges, not at a uniquely reproducible point, so the shape numbers are downgraded to **low confidence / ±60 m, ±30 m, ±60%** respectively (was ±20–30%); the independently text-sourced ~10,000 m² area (R1, R4) is the more reliable figure. A separate automated centroid re-check placed the reef's offshore distance at ~195 m — on the low side of, but broadly consistent with, the 210–225 m text range given the same wide measurement uncertainty.
2. **`Boscombe-Exposed-1024x683.jpg`** (raisedwaterresearch.com, same page). Downloaded, viewed, deleted. Ground-level photo showing the sand-covered geobags breaching the surface at low tide — this is a direct visual confirmation of R10's "0.5 m above low-springs sea-level" design claim (the crest is emergent, not submerged). The photo shows what look like two parallel raised ridges with a shallow trough between them; this is noted as a possible sign of a two-layer/two-row construction (consistent with R10's "constructed in two layers") but was not used to change the overall "linear/single-ramp" shape classification, since it could equally be crest-vs-toe of a single ramp seen from a low oblique angle.
3. **`Boscombe_Surf_Reef.jpg`** (Wikimedia Commons, R15). Downloaded, viewed, deleted. Captioned by Wikipedia as "Diagram showing layout of the geotextile bags" — turned out to be a simplified cartoon (a side/perspective row of tube cross-sections forming a ramp, stylised waves and sand, no scale, no plan view). **Not usable for any dimension or plan-view shape**; recorded here so future passes don't re-fetch it expecting a real drawing.
4. `wavelength-mag` photo (already in card, credited to Flickr user "cornerhouse" via Wavelength Surf Mag, R5) was also viewed for context (shows two similar parallel emergent ridges as image 2) but not separately measured.

No new photo/video searches were performed; all four images above were already listed in the card's `03_images/web/boscombe-surf-reef.json`.

## Polygon construction

Coordinate convention: origin = the shoreline point directly onshore of the reef's photo-measured centroid; **x = alongshore** (positive east, matching the card's "~240 m east of the pier" framing); **y = offshore** (positive seaward); shoreline ≈ the line y = 0.

Construction steps:
1. In the aerial photo, picked the four corners of the reef's dark patch and Boscombe Pier's base and tip, in pixel coordinates.
2. Derived scale (1.096 m/px) from the pier's known real-world length (180 m, S1) divided by its pixel length (164.3 px).
3. Fitted the reef outline as a simple rotated rectangle: long axis 187.5 m (bearing ~247° offshore), width 58 m, centred at (alongshore 0, offshore 210 m) — i.e. directly offshore of the origin by construction.
4. Computed the four rectangle corners from the centre, half-length/half-width and the axis unit vectors, then converted pixel-derived offsets to the coordinate convention above.
5. Closed the polygon (first vertex repeated) and verified with shapely that it is `is_valid` and `is_simple` (non-self-intersecting); shoelace/shapely area = 10,861.6 m², consistent with the independently text-sourced ~10,000 m² (1 hectare) figure within measurement uncertainty.

This is deliberately a simplified rectangle, not a traced freeform outline — the source photo's resolution and water-blur do not support confidently placing more than 4 vertices. `bbox_m` in the JSON additionally reports the axis-aligned envelope (195 m alongshore × 127.6 m offshore), which is much larger than the rectangle's own 187.5 m × 58 m because the ramp is tilted ~24° off the shore-normal — a reminder that "alongshore length" and "the structure's own length" are not the same thing for an angled reef like this one.

## Open issues

- No real engineering plan-view drawing, bathymetric/multibeam survey, or as-built report was located online for this reef (search engines returned CAPTCHA/JS walls during this pass; only already-cited pages and their images were consulted, per task rules). If Lior can supply a PDF of the University of Plymouth Davidson (2010) evaluation or the Rendle & Davidson (2012, ICCE) paper mentioned in the card, it likely contains a real plan-view figure that would sharply improve confidence here.
- Container/bag count and size conflict across sources (R7: 55 bags, 2–3 m high, 60 m long, ~1,000 t total; R10: 56 bags, 50–70 m long, two layers, biggest bags 2.5 m × 5 m). Not reconciled.
- Area is reported as "one hectare" (R1, R4) vs. "a football pitch" (R10, notably smaller); we preferred the hectare figure (two agreeing sources + photo cross-check).
- Offshore distance ranges from 210 m to 250 m across sources depending on article; we used the 210–225 m cluster (three sources) and treated the isolated 250 m figure as an outlier.
- Our photo-derived long-axis bearing (~247°) assumes the aerial photo is north-up; this was inferred from the pier's visible direction, not confirmed against a map or EXIF data.
- All photo-based shape numbers (length, width, bearing) carry meaningful uncertainty from image blur/resolution; only the area, volume, and offshore-distance figures are solidly text-sourced. **[UPDATED 2026-09-25]** An adversarial re-measurement found this uncertainty is substantially larger than the ±20–30% originally stated — see "Verification 2026-09-25" below for the full re-measurement and its ~2–8x sensitivity to threshold choice. Treat 187.5 m × 58 m as a rough, order-of-magnitude sketch consistent with a ~1 ha elongated bank, not a validated measurement.

## Verification 2026-09-25

Adversarial re-check of the 2026-09-25 research pass above. Every cited source was re-fetched live (WebFetch) and every cited image was re-downloaded and viewed directly; the polygon was independently recomputed with shapely; the reef and pier were independently re-measured from the same aerial photo using automated pixel-darkness thresholding (not manual corner-picking) at multiple threshold levels to test how sensitive the original corner-picking method is. Gemini's own source files were read directly to check the fairness of the Gemini-comparison table.

**Findings**

1. **Text sources — all confirmed.** R1 (Wikipedia), R4 (Raised Water Research), R7 (Bournemouth Swell) and R10 (Great British Life) were each re-fetched and every quoted sentence in this file matches the live page verbatim (hectare/2.5-acre area, 13,000 m³ volume, 225 m / 210 m / 220 m offshore figures, bag counts and dimensions, "0.5 m above low springs" crest, ride lengths, the "ramp not wave-maker" quote). R3 (no dimensions) and R9 (propeller-strike only, no dimensions) also confirmed as claimed. S1 (Boscombe Pier, "200 yards (180 m) long") confirmed exactly.
2. **Error found and fixed — R2/R4 quote mix-up.** The "Sources and what each gave" list wrongly quoted R4's "2.5 acres (1 hectare)… about 210m from the shoreline" under the **R2** heading. A live fetch of R2's actual URL shows it contains no "hectare", "acres" or "210 m" anywhere — R2's real content is "positioned 250m offshore" and a football-pitch size comparison. Fixed above; the JSON's own R2 reference-list entry was already correct and did not need changing.
3. **Error found and fixed — mis-cited offshore-distance ref.** The card's headline "220 m from the seawall base" figure was tagged to R4 in the JSON's `distance_offshore_m` field, but R4's own text says 210 m. The source that literally states "220m from the base of the seawall" is **R7**. Fixed in the JSON (`ref` changed R4→R7) and in the Derivation table above; also corrected the "majority value" phrasing (only one of the three real sources — R7 — literally states 220 m; R1 and R4 differ).
4. **Error found and fixed — footprint-area over-citation.** The JSON's derivation row for the 10,000 m² footprint area cited "R1,R4,R7" as sources, but R7's page contains no area/hectare/acreage statement at all (only bag dimensions and distance). Fixed to "R1,R4".
5. **Polygon arithmetic — confirmed.** Recomputing the JSON's `polygon_m` with shapely reproduces `area_m2` (10,861.6 m²) and `bbox_m` (195.0 m × 127.6 m) exactly, and its two long/short side pairs measure 187.4 m and 58.0 m — consistent with `max_dimension_m` and the stated width. The polygon is correctly built from the stated L×W; the concern below is with those inputs, not this arithmetic.
6. **Photo-derived shape numbers — confidence lowered, uncertainty widened.** Independently re-measuring the same aerial photo with automated (threshold-based, not manual) pixel darkness detection reproduced the pier's pixel length well (~162 px vs. the original 164.3 px, ~1.5% apart — a solid confirmation of the ~1.1 m/px scale bar). But the reef patch itself is genuinely diffuse/water-blurred: testing 5 plausible darkness thresholds for "where the patch ends" produced major-axis lengths of ~98–170 px (~110–190 m), minor-axis widths of ~18–83 px (~20–92 m), and footprint areas of ~2,200–17,300 m² — an ~8× spread purely from threshold choice. The original 187.5 m / 58 m / 10,861.6 m² figures sit near the top of these ranges and are retained (nothing is invented to replace them), but their stated precision (±20–30%) understated the true measurement uncertainty; uncertainty has been widened (±60 m / ±30 m / ±60%) and confidence on these three numbers specifically has been lowered to **low**. An automated centroid re-check also gave an offshore distance of ~195 m, versus the original pass's ~208–210 m — both are within the method's now-wider error bars, but this is a further sign the photo method is less precise than originally presented. The well text-sourced numbers (area ≈1 ha, volume, 210–225 m offshore range, crest above datum) are unaffected and remain medium confidence.
7. **Crest-depth sign convention — clarified, not a factual change.** The JSON stored the crest level as `-0.5` with a note that negative meant *above* datum — technically correct but confusing next to Gemini's own sign convention. Changed to `+0.5` with an unambiguous "positive = above datum / emergent" note; the underlying fact (crest sits 0.5 m above LAT/spring-low per R10, corroborated by the "Exposed" photo) is unchanged.
8. **Shape type vs. Gemini's "triangular delta" — confirmed.** Direct viewing of the aerial photo shows one coherent, tapering dark patch with no distinct two-armed apex, supporting "linear" over Gemini's "asymmetric triangular delta / right-hand peeler". The Wikimedia diagram was independently re-viewed and is confirmed to be a non-scaled, 312×139 px side-view cartoon, not a plan-view survey. The "Exposed" photo was independently re-viewed and confirms two parallel emergent ridges at low tide.
9. **Gemini comparison — confirmed fair.** Gemini's own files (`reef_footprints.json`, `reef_footprint_scale_analysis.md`, `scale_sketch_photo_alignment_audit.md`) were read directly. Every Gemini figure and quote attributed to it in this file's "Gemini comparison" table (100 m × 55 m, 5,200 m² gross / 3,070 m² net core, crest depth 1.5 m / "-1.0 m LAT", the three named but URL-less sources) is an accurate representation of what Gemini's own files actually contain.
10. **Sand-fill volume ref — corrected for completeness.** The JSON's top-level `volume_m3.ref` listed only "R4"; R1 states the identical figure too, so it now reads "R1,R4" (the Derivation table already had this right).

**Net effect on confidence**: the reef's classification (single elongated "linear" bank/ramp, ~1 hectare footprint, ~210–225 m offshore, crest ~0.5 m above LAT/spring-low, ~13,000 m³ sand) remains **medium confidence**, now with a cleaner, re-verified source trail. The specific plan-view numbers carried in `max_dimension_m` (187.5 m), the width (58 m), the "photo cross-check" area (10,861.6 m²), and the bearing (~245–250°) are retained (no invented replacement values) but downgraded to **low confidence** with substantially widened uncertainty bands, per the JSON's `verification` array.
