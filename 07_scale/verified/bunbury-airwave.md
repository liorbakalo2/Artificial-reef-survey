# Bunbury Airwave — Footprint Research

Status: verified (2026-09-25)

## Summary

The Bunbury Airwave was an inflatable, dome-shaped rubber ("Hypalon") bladder — not a rock/concrete structure — so its "footprint" is simply the circular base it occupies on the sand. All primary sources agree it was a **circular dome, 12 m in base diameter**, rising **2 m off the seabed at its highest point** (one later retrospective article gives 1.6 m instead). Footprint area is therefore a **plain circle, π×6² ≈ 113 m²**. It was designed to sit **30–45 m offshore** in shallow water where, at low tide, "the shallow point is about 1 m under the surface of the water" — i.e. **crest depth ≈ 1.0 m below the low-tide surface**, not fully emergent but very shallow. Because the dome is a circle, it has **no meaningful "orientation"** — length and width are identical and there are no arms/apex to point in a direction. Confidence: **medium-high** for shape/diameter/height (directly stated, multiple sources, corroborated by an aerial drone photo already in our card); **medium** for crest depth and offshore distance (stated but as a range/approximate figure, and never verified as-installed because the structure tore during installation and was removed before completion).

## Polygon construction

- **Coordinate convention:** origin at the shoreline point nearest the reef's centroid; x = alongshore, taken positive toward north (Bunbury Back Beach's shoreline trends approximately north–south, consistent with the aerial photo's framing in our card — this is a coarse geographic approximation, not a surveyed bearing, since no source in our references gives an exact shoreline azimuth); y = offshore, positive seaward; shoreline approximated as the line y = 0.
- Since the plan shape is a circle, alongshore/offshore orientation has no effect on the polygon's shape — only its center's y-coordinate (offshore distance) matters.
- **Center:** (x=0, y=37.5) — placed at the midpoint of the stated 30–45 m design offshore-distance range, with x=0 (no alongshore offset is documented; the reef was sited directly off a single point on Back Beach near the surf club).
- **Radius:** 6.0 m (half of the stated 12 m diameter).
- **Vertices:** 24 equally-spaced points around the circle (10° increments), closed by repeating the first vertex as the last (25 coordinate pairs total). This is a discretization for storage in the polygon_m array — the true plan shape is a smooth circle, so `area_m2` in the JSON is computed from the exact circle formula (113.1 m²), not from the 24-gon's slightly smaller shoelace area (≈111.8 m², a ~1% polygonal-approximation artifact, not a data disagreement).
- **Validation:** checked with `shapely` (re-run 2026-09-25, v2.1.2) — `Polygon.is_valid == True`, `exterior.is_simple == True` (closed, non-self-intersecting, 25 coordinate pairs incl. the repeated first/last vertex). Bbox independently computed with python from `polygon_m`: x ∈ [-6.0, 6.0] (12.0 m), y ∈ [31.5, 43.5] (12.0 m) — exactly matches the stated 12 m × 12 m and the `bbox_m` field in the JSON.

## Open issues

- The Airwave was never completed: installation reached ~90% before a glued seam tore, and the bladder was removed within about three days (one conflicting source says half a day) [R1][R5][R8][R18]. Every dimension here is therefore an **as-designed** value; no as-installed or as-surveyed measurement exists for this reef.
- Height above the seabed is inconsistently reported: 2 m in three contemporaneous sources ([R1][R2][R3]) vs 1.6 m in one 2022 retrospective article [R8]; [R6] only gives a loose "about 7-feet" (2.13 m) approximation. We default to 2 m (majority, contemporaneous, precise) in the summary but the JSON records both.
- [R3] and [R9] in the card both cite the same raisedwaterresearch.com URL under two different reference ids/citation texts — likely a small duplication in the original card's reference list, not a new source; flagged here for awareness, not corrected in the card per this task's scope (footprint file only).
- No source gives an exact installed lat/lon or a surveyed offshore distance; the 30–45 m figure is the pre-installation design target from R3 only ([R2] does not mention any offshore distance — corrected 2026-09-25).
- No source states a footprint/base volume directly; the ≈117 m³ figure here is our own geometric estimate (spherical cap), offered only as a plausibility cross-check against the stated 140-tonne sand fill, not as a published number.
- Gemini's two cited sources ("Bottegal 2019 Project Brief", "Surfer Magazine Trial Review 2020") could not be located or verified in our own research and do not appear in our card's reference list; Gemini's crest-depth and offshore-distance figures should be treated as unverified estimates, not independently sourced numbers.
- The main reef card's `unexpected_results` field paraphrases the R8 wave-peel quote as "peeled nearly all the way to the beach"; the actual R8 text (re-fetched 2026-09-25) says "peeled for about thirty metres." This is a wording discrepancy in the card, noted here for awareness but out of scope to fix in this footprint-only task (it does not affect any footprint dimension).
