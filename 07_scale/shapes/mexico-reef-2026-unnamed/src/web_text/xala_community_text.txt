Xala, Jalisco: Where Nature, Culture, and Purpose Unite
Where is Xala?				
Tucked along five miles of Mexico’s Pacific Coast in Costalegre, Jalisco, Xala is a true coastal sanctuary. Located about 65 miles south of Puerto Vallarta – just north of Careyes and only 10 minutes from the new Costalegre airport – Xala is both remote and remarkably accessible.
Nesteled between two estuaries, this beautiful destination is home to 440 acres of organic mango orchards, surrounded by vibrant coastal jungle vegetation and framed by the Sierra Madre mountains and pristine beaches.								
Our Values				
Xala embodies a living philosophy where purpose and character intertwine. Our shared values nurture the soul and heal the land – forming the beating heart of this sacred endeavor.								
					Consciousness				
We are mindful of our role in the world – and our place within it. 
Xala is a living story of regeneration, showing how thoughtful development can enrich all forms of life. Xala, Jalisco inspires consciousness and cultivates a thriving, nature-aligned community.								
					Respect				
We honor nature´s gifts and take responsibility for their protection. 
From mangroves to estuaries, each ecosystem is restored and stewarded with care – preserving the land’s legacy for future generations. 								
					Generosity				
We embrace the spirit of giving – from locals to visitors and visitors to locals. 
In Xala, community is one: shared, inclusive, indivisible. 
Here, generosity becomes its own reward, cultivating deep connection across the vibrant Costalegre region.								
					Creativity				
We celebrate creativity as a natural outcome of living in harmony with the land. 
The peace and raw beauty that surround Xala nourish the soul and awaken the senses – inviting pause, reflection and original thought.
Here, inspiration flows freely.
From artistic expression to bold ideas and meaningful breakthroughs, Xala´s tranquil-setting becomes a quiet spark of extraordinary creativity.
