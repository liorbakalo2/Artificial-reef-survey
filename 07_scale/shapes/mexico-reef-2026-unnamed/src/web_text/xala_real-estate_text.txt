Explore Real Estate Opportunities in Costalegre, Jalisco
Xala is a curated masterpiece of sustainable living–where community, environmental restoration and natural beauty are seamlessly interwoven.
Nestled along the Pacific Coast in the enchanting Costalegre, Jalisco (known as the Happy Coast), just 65 miles south of Puerto Vallarta, Xala spans 3,000 acres of untouched natural wonder.
With five miles of pristine coastline, two Ramsar estuaries, and 440 acres of organic mango orchards, Xala is a sanctuary where elevated living and nature live in perfect harmony.
Accessing Xala is effortless–just a short 25-minute flight from Puerto Vallarta via the new Costalegre Airport. From the moment you arrive, Xala invites you to embark on an extraordinary journey.

Luxury Beachfront Homes in Costalegre, Jalisco 				
With just 120 residences spread across 3,000 acres, Xala redefines refined living through space, privacy, and untouched natural splendor.
Among Xala’s signature offerings are 75 Rancho Estates—beautiful real estate investment properties ready to become exquisitely crafted ocean front homes. Each five-acre lot features 192 linear feet of beachfront and awe-inspiring, 180-degree ocean views.
With a curated roster of pre-approved architects, builders and design teams, bringing your vision to life in Costalegre is effortless. These private Ranchos offer a rare opportunity for those seeking a legacy property in one of Mexico´s most extraordinary destinations.
