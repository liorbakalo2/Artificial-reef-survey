# METHODS_3D - Narrowneck Reef, Gold Coast (Queensland, Australia)

Written 2026-10-10 (stage 2, 3D agent). Reproduce with `python prep_multibeam.py`, `python build_3d.py`, `python make_annotations.py` in this folder. Step log: SOURCES_3D.md. Source audit 2026-10-10 (documentation only, no model value changed): SOURCE_AUDIT_2026-10-10.md in the parent folder; sections 2b, 3.6, 3.7, 3.8 and 6 below were added or extended by it.

## 1. Summary
The model shows the **renewed Narrowneck Reef as left in June 2018** (84 containers added around the 2004-revised structure; about 534 containers placed since 1999). The default plan is the **visible container field on the Esri aerial of 8 Aug 2020** (6,239 m2, 7 polygons; a lower bound because the deeper toe is not visible). Four more outlines are selectable (Option 2 renewal design 2017, 2004 revised design, 2011 survey relief zone, Navionics 4.0 m shoals). Heights come from three measured items: the crest (**-2.2 m AHD**, 2018 multibeam, Vieira da Silva et al. 2021), the 2017 pre-works multibeam of Corbett et al. (2023) Fig 3 (read from its colour scale: seabed -4 m at the shoreward ends of the arms, -7 m at the toe, container relief 1-2 m), and the design contours (crest -2.5 m AHD falling to -6 m). **Default outline = the 2020 photo trace: decision D7 by Lior, 2026-10-10** (section 3.8). **3D confidence: MEDIUM** (section 6): the plan is a georeferenced photo trace and the heights are measured, but the multibeam is read from a colour picture (+-0.4 m, placed to +-10 m), no post-renewal plan or survey exists, and the crest datum differs by 0.24 m between sources. Reef height is known to about +-1 m; the volumes are mound envelopes (including gaps between containers), not sand-fill volumes.

State and history (not modelled, notes only): 408 containers placed Aug 1999 - Dec 2000 (crest first -1.5 AHD, top-ups to -1.0 LAT in 2001, then lowered to -2.5 AHD); +10 (2002-03), +15 in 2004 (weir and flared wings, the 2004 revised design), +17 (2006); settlement 1-2 m locally (weir containers lowered up to 2 m); renewal 2017-18 with 84 containers (Option 2 layout; the north reef almost fully achieved, the south reef and bridge only partly); crest measured -2.2 m AHD after it. No later change is documented. Sources: Jackson et al. 2007, 2012; Corbett et al. 2023; City of Gold Coast 2020.

## 3. Methods
### 3.1 Frame and datums
- Frame: shape.json canonical (stage 1): origin = waterline of the 8 Aug 2020 image nearest the reef centroid (-27.986800, 153.431127); **x alongshore toward bearing 356.21 deg (north)**, **y offshore toward bearing 86.21 deg (east)**; z up, 0 = MSL; metres.
- Datum chain (Gold Coast Seaway, MSQ): AHD = LAT + 0.760 m, MSL = LAT + 0.88 m, hence **z_MSL = z_AHD - 0.12 = z_LAT - 0.88**. Planes above LAT: MLWS 0.22, MLWN 0.51, MSL 0.88, MHWN 1.24, MHWS 1.53, HAT 2.03; with z = 0 at MSL: LAT -0.88, MLWS -0.66, MLWN -0.37, MHWN +0.36, MHWS +0.65, HAT +1.15 m. Local tides only (rule 5); the Snapper Rocks row (MSL 1.09) is not used.
- **Crest datum decision (open issue, 0.24 m).** The papers (Jackson 2007 p.7, Corbett 2023 p.3, council layer) write the same design crest as "-1.5 m LAT (RL -2.5 m AHD)", i.e. AHD = LAT + 1.00 m; MSQ gives AHD = LAT + 0.760 m. **This model uses MSQ.** Reasons: (1) it is the official dated benchmark relation for the nearest standard port, (2) the papers' pairs are rounded, come from one author group and state no derivation, (3) every height here is measured in m AHD (multibeam, Vieira da Silva, design RL), so the choice does **not** change the geometry; it only moves reef and seabed 0.24 m against LAT/MSL/tide planes. Consequences for the crest: measured -2.2 m AHD = **1.44 m below LAT (MSQ) or 1.20 m (papers)**; design -2.5 m AHD = 1.74 m (MSQ) or 1.50 m (papers). The viewer has a switch that applies the papers' rule (geometry +0.24 m). Resolution: ask the City or MSQ for the offset used in the 2018 survey.
- All values were read in AHD; "assumed AHD" is stated where a figure does not name its datum (Corbett Fig 3, Jackson Fig 2). Support: the shallowest container tops of Fig 3 read -1.8 (south arm) and -2.5 m (north arm) vs the measured crest -2.2 m AHD.

### 3.2 Reef surface
1. **Multibeam raster (Corbett Fig 3, `prep_multibeam.py`).** Scale: the 20 m bar has ticks at x = 274, 295, 317 px: 0.4651 m/px (+-1.2 %); the map is 352 x 312 px = 163.7 x 145.1 m. Depth: the colour bar (x 383-426 px) is fitted linearly through its four tick labels (-1, -4, -6, -9 at y = 140, 198, 253, 305 px; residuals 0.03, -0.23, 0.37, -0.17 m); each map pixel is converted by its **hue** (the figure is hill-shaded, so brightness is ignored): `depth = -0.0472 y + 5.58`, hue monotonic from red (-1.7 m) to blue (-7.9 m). Pixels deeper than -7.9 m or paler than -1.7 m are clipped. Bed: grey-scale opening (erosion then dilation, disk radius 10 px = 4.65 m, removes containers narrower than 9 m) and Gaussian smoothing (6 px); relief R = surface - bed (median 0.1 m, p90 0.7 m, p99 1.7 m).
2. **Registration.** Assumed north-up with no rotation. Translation chosen by maximum overlap of relief > 0.6 m with the traced 2020 arm polygons (soft mask): image centre at canonical (x -5, y 290) m, +-10 m (the score maximum is broad, 0.52 against 0.51 at 5 m offsets; the south arm fits best; the container field in 2017 differs from 2020 by the 84 added containers). See A1.
3. **Design surface (`nn_lib.design_top`).** Each design (Option 2, 2004) has crest loops (-2.5 m AHD contour) and an outer envelope (-6 m AHD). Inside the envelope the top level is `z = -2.5 - 3.5 f`, `f = d_in / (d_in + d_out)` with `d_in` the distance to the crest loops and `d_out` the distance to the envelope boundary (1 m raster, Euclidean distance transform). The contours between (-3 ... -5) are NOT digitised; they are replaced by this linear rule (check in 4.2).
4. **Default version (photo).** Top = Option 2 design surface lowered to the measured crest (-2.2 m AHD, i.e. +0.3 m), at least 1.0 m above the bed (the containers are visible, so at least one layer; multibeam relief 1-2 m), isolated containers (channel containers, wing patches) 1.8 m above the bed, no top shallower than -1.8 m AHD (the shallowest multibeam value). Footprint = the 7 traced polygons. Side slope: height rises 0.67 m per m inward from the polygon edge (+0.3 m at the edge), i.e. 1.5 H : 1 V (assumption A5).
5. **Design versions.** Same rule at the design crest -2.5 m AHD with the Option 2 loops and envelope (version 2), the 2004 loops and envelope (version 3), and the 2004 surface cut to the 2011 relief zone (version 4, an assumption). No minimum height is applied: where the design surface lies below the bed the mound height is zero.
6. **Navionics shoals** (version 5): outline only, no solid, because the chart shoals are smoothed (4.0 m over a reef whose crest is -2.2 m AHD = 1.44 m below LAT) and are not a footprint.
7. **Grid.** Reef grid 1 m (x -110..110, y 190..420), stored in cm as int16 relative to MSL.

### 3.3 Seabed
- Window (about 164 x 145 m around the reef): the opened multibeam bed (3.2), Gaussian 3 m, blended into the surrounding profile over 15 m from the window edge.
- Alongshore-uniform profile b(y) (PCHIP, `nn_lib.seabed_profile`), m AHD: **window part** = median over x of the multibeam bed (y 215-365); **seaward** Navionics SonarChart labels 7, 8, 9, 10 m read on img-50 at y of about 402, 411, 474, 514 m (map centre = reef centroid +-20 m, 0.4316 m per displayed px, 3.8 deg rotation corrected), converted AHD = -(depth LAT) - 0.76 = -7.76, -8.76, -9.76, -10.76 (LAT assumed; consistent with Vieira da Silva Fig 3 "-9/-10 seaward"), then flat to -11.0 at 540 m; **shoreward** ASSUMED: 0.0 m at the waterline (y = 0), -1.8 at y = 100 (mean of the Vieira Fig 3 reading -1..-2 and Navionics), -2.1 at y = 190 (the window edge value), +1.2 at y = -40 (beach).
- Grid 5 m, x -250..250, y -40..520; alongshore constant outside the window (Vieira da Silva Fig 3 shows an alongshore-uniform bed over 1.5 km).
- Not used: Navionics nearshore contours (its land edge lies about 80 m seaward of our 2020 waterline, so its surf-zone contours are not in our frame); the Navionics "0.9" spot depth (obstruction label, older crest state; Lior's reading is pending).

### 3.4 Versions and volumes
Volume = sum over the 1 m grid of (reef top - bed) inside the footprint. It is a **mound envelope**: it includes the gaps between containers and ignores containers buried below the modelled bed, so it is not the sand-fill volume. Bed uncertainty +-0.5 m over the footprint gives the stated +-.

### 3.5 Water, tides and viewer
Water plane at the selected level (LAT to HAT slider, chips for the MSQ planes and AHD); vertical exaggeration 1-5; datum switch (3.1); other outlines as lines draped on the seabed; faint seaward patches (24 patches, 663 m2, stage 1) as pink lines (not in any outline); photo-match mode (camera position, heading, pitch, FOV; copy camera JSON); presets: oblique from the sea, plan (north = right, shore = top), along the north arm, from the beach at eye height 1.7 m, side view.

### 3.7 Other methods, tools and AI involvement
- Scripts that produced numbers (all in this folder or the parent folder; each can be re-run): `../trace_s3.py` (trace and shoreline), `../design_registration.py` (Fig 7 on s3), `../verify_scripts/` (Option 2 placement, 2004 outline, 2011 zone, faint patches, Navionics loops), `prep_multibeam.py` (colour to depth, bed), `nn_lib.py` (design surface, bed profile), `build_3d.py` (volumes, validation numbers, model.js, docs.js), `make_annotations.py`.
- Numerical methods: threshold + morphological closing (plan); normalised cross-correlation and phase correlation (image registration); chamfer fit (Corbett Option 1 on Jackson Fig 2: one isotropic scale, residual 0.65 px); Euclidean distance transform (design surface); grey opening and Gaussian filter (bed); PCHIP interpolation (bed profile); sums over a 1 m grid (volumes). The 5 m seabed grid is bilinear in the viewer.
- Reading by eye (labelled as such): Navionics labels, Vieira da Silva Fig 3 colours, Jackson Fig 2 contour labels, Corbett tick labels. Each image was viewed once, downscaled.
- AI: the model was built by Claude agents (stage 1 trace and verification; stage 2 3D; this audit). Their own earlier estimate of reef height (4-6 m at the toe) was checked against the multibeam and NOT supported (4.3); it is not used. Gemini (another AI) supplied nothing that is used: its footprint (two lobes, 400 x 220 m, 75,000 m2) was rejected after we fetched or read the originals (HANDOFF section 9). Re-fetching the Gemini-cited pages was not possible (no network access to the hosts); Black and Mead 2001 is paywalled.

### 4.1 The five outline versions compared

The photo footprint lies 96.6 % inside the Option 2 envelope and 94.5 % inside the 2004 envelope (stage 1). Option 2 holds 1.6 x the 2004 volume because it sits about 22 m further seaward on a deeper bed. The photo volume is a **lower bound for the footprint** (toe invisible) but an **envelope** for the material (gaps filled); the two biases have opposite signs, so no net sign is claimed. Decision D7 (default photo vs Option 2): **decided by Lior, 2026-10-10: the photo is the default**; Option 2 stays a toggle (the only evidence of the state actually built is the photo).

### 4.2 Design surface vs the 2017 multibeam tops
Blocks of 8 x 8 m inside the 2004 envelope containing relief > 0.8 m (n = 53): the 90th percentile of the multibeam depth per block vs the design surface at the block centre: **mean offset +0.26 m (design shallower), sd 0.66 m, correlation 0.76**. A positive offset is expected (settlement 1-2 m since 2004); an exponent 0.7 instead of 1 would give +0.02 m (not used: no tuning). Shallowest multibeam tops: north arm -2.51, south arm -1.82 m AHD (p99 -3.13 and -2.72) vs measured crest -2.2 and target -2.5.

### 4.3 Reef height
Relief above the opened bed: north arm p90 0.92 m, p99 1.81 m; south arm p90 1.22 m, p99 2.22 m (A1 right panel). The stage-1 estimate (2-3 m at the shoreward crest, 4-6 m at the toe) is **not supported at the toe**: the multibeam bed at the toe is -6.5 to -7.5 m and container tops there -5 to -6, so the relief is 1-1.5 m; at the crest wedge top -2.2 to -3 on bed -4 to -5 gives 2-2.5 m. The opening leaves wide stacks in the bed, so these heights are biased low (the model arms have mean height 2.16 m, maximum 3.9 m at the Option 2 north loop on -6 m bed).

### 4.4 Seabed and Navionics
Model bed along y (m AHD): 0 at y = 0, -1.8 at 100, -2.1 at 190, -3.9 at 235, -5.1 at 265, -5.8 at 300, -6.8 at 340, -7.3 at 370, -7.7 at 400, -9.6 at 460, -10.5 at 500. Vieira da Silva Fig 3 (A3): inner edge -4/-5 (model -3.9 to -5.1 at y 235-265), under the reef -6 to -8 (model -5.8 to -7.3), seaward -9/-10 (model -9.6 at 460): consistent within +-1 m. Navionics (A2, A5): 4.0-4.5 m LAT over the arms = -4.8 to -5.3 AHD against the model bed -5.1 to -5.8 and crest -2.2 to -4: the chart smooths the reef into a shoal and shows holes of 6 m between; 5.5-6 m around the arms = -6.3 to -6.8 AHD against the model -5 to -6.8 (the multibeam bed is up to 1.5 m shallower); the seaward labels are the data used. Its land edge sits 80 m seaward of our waterline: unresolved; the nearshore profile (A7) remains an assumption.

### 4.5 Container count vs volume
534 containers x 147 m3 of sand each = 78,500 m3, while the model mound envelope is 13.5k (photo) to 21.3k m3 (Option 2): 17-27 %. Also the footprint of 534 containers (20 x 4 m) would be about 43,000 m2 against 6,239 m2 visible and 13,941 m2 in the design envelope. Containers are therefore buried, stacked below the visible field, displaced or lost: **the model shows only what the photo and multibeam show**, not the full placed volume.
