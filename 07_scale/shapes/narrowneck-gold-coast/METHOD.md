## Step 2a - PRIMARY trace on s3 (Esri Wayback 2020-08-08), done 2026-10-06
Method (reproducible; script saved as trace_s3.py in this folder):
1. Green channel of s3 (red is clipped at 33, green separates containers best: containers ~41-45, water 55-75 in the west half), Gaussian blur 1 px, threshold G < 49 inside the window x 240-880, y 500-1020 (raw px of the 1517 px image). Threshold chosen by looking at G < 46 / 49 / 52 contours over the stretched image: 49 follows the visible container edges; 52 starts to bleed into the darker deep water east of x ~ 800.
2. 21 connected components (area >= 60 px) were listed and numbered; ids and bounding boxes are in the trace script output. Morphological closing with an elliptical kernel of radius 14 px (3.7 m) bridges the gaps between neighbouring containers of one arm (container spacing inside the arms 1-5 m) and was chosen over r = 8 (leaves the arm as a dozen fragments) and r = 22 (merges the arms' satellites). Douglas-Peucker 3 px (0.8 m).
3. Polygons: north_arm (71 vertices, 3,679 m2), south_arm (51, 2,270 m2; a looser G < 53 was applied in one box to include the SW container of the arm), two shoreward patches NW of the north arm (129 and 105 m2), three weir containers in the channel (26, 19, 13 m2; the second and third are faint and were read by eye at 6x zoom). Total traced area 6,239 m2 over 7 polygons.
4. Not in polygons (listed in shape.json "extras_not_in_polygons"): three isolated dark patches 130-170 px east of the north arm tip in deeper water (raw ~ x 767-880, y 539-664). They may be partly buried/deep containers or natural reef; the 2011 aerial (Fig 7) also shows a dark band there. Small isolated dark spots < 60 px2 were ignored.
