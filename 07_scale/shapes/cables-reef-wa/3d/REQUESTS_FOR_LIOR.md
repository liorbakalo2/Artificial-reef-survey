# REQUESTS_FOR_LIOR - Cable Station reef 3D model (cables-reef-wa, 2026-10-10)

Nothing here blocks the model: it is built from the printed design drawing with the stated assumptions (LOW 3D confidence). Each row is something the sandbox could not read (network blocked). The reef has no surveyed coordinates in our sources (the card has only 'about 32.00 S, 115.74 E', a generic surf-spot position), so points are given by place and by the model frame: x alongshore (+ south), y offshore (+ west), metres from the shoreline point nearest the reef centroid; the centroid is at (0, 270). Reef landmarks in that frame: outer apex (-11, 314), inner apex (-12, 265), south arm tip (61, 248), north arm tip (-60, 268), valley between the arms (-38, 254), corner at the south arm (27, 238).
