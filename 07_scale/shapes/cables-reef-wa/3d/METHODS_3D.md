# METHODS_3D - Cable Station artificial surfing reef (cables-reef-wa)

First run 2026-10-10 (design drawing only, no imagery); REVISED 2026-10-10 (imagery + bathymetry run, section 11 lists every change; the first-run files are in `archive/pre_imagery_2026-10-10/`). Re-generate model.js / docs.js with `python3 build_3d.py` (needs ../shape.json, ../scripts/computed.json, ../scripts/a2_trace.json, ../scripts/georef.json, survey_volume.json, shore_datum.json, bed_measured_canonical.npz, ../src/bathymetry/, METHODS_3D.md, SOURCES_3D.md). Annotated sources: `annotated/`, `../src/imagery/annotated/`, `../src/bathymetry/figures/`. Viewer: `index.html`.

## 1. Summary
The model shows the Cable Station reef (built Feb-Dec 1999) in six versions. DEFAULT = the rock surface surveyed by the Western Australian Department of Transport (DoT) in Nov 1999 (840 single-beam soundings, m AHD), on the V-shaped footprint of the Lyons (1992) design plan (121 m alongshore x 76 m cross-shore, 4,049 m2) that was georeferenced by fitting it to those soundings, over the MEASURED pre-construction seabed (DoT survey ending 30 Mar 1998). Also shown: the Feb 2002 surveyed surface, the design surface (ramp RL -3.0 to -1.0, side slopes to the bed) without and with the drawn 347 m2 extension of the south arm (4,355 m2), and two plan-only outlines (paper Fig 7, Pitt concept sketch). Rock volume (m3): surveyed 4,671 (Nov 1999) and 5,219 (Feb 2002), +-1,000; design surface on the measured bed 3,920 (original) / 4,249 (with extension); stated 3,500 / 5,000 / 5,500. Remaining modelled or assumed items: the design surface between the printed contours (A10), the side slopes (A5), the datum link RL - AHD (A1, A12; the soundings imply 0.5-0.6 m, reading A 0.78 m), the extension (A6, not confirmed), tide range symmetry (A7). 3D confidence: MEDIUM (was LOW).

## 3. Methods
### 3.1 Frames and datum
- shape.json canonical frame: x alongshore (+ north), y offshore (+ west), origin on the shoreline with the original-plan centroid at y = 271 m. Model frame (model.js): x_model = -x_canonical (+ south-south-east) so that (x, y west, z up) is right-handed in three.js. Since 2026-10-10 the frame is rotated by theta = 9.74 deg counter-clockwise from true north (georeference, 3.2): canonical +x has bearing 350.3 deg T, the apex points to 260.3 deg T. The first model assumed the shoreline N-S; the measured shoreline bearing is 7.8 deg T (3.6).
- Heights of the design drawing are RL (reduced level of the design datum). Reading A (default): RL is the tide-gauge / chart datum (Pattiaratchi 1999 and the Pitt note: 'datum', '0.0 on the tide gauge'); mean tide level 0.78 m above it (Pattiaratchi 1999 p.3; BoM Fremantle record: 0.77 m, 3.6): z_MSL = z_RL - 0.78. Reading B: Blacka (2013) 'crest submergence 1-2.5 m below MSL': z_MSL = z_RL.
- Surveyed heights are m AHD (DoT). The model's z axis is m relative to MSL; AHD is taken as MSL (assumption A12). Bed and surveyed surfaces therefore need no datum choice; only the DESIGN versions depend on A/B. In the viewer reading B moves the water plane relative to the design versions; the surveyed versions ignore it.
- Datum test with the soundings (build_3d.py, validation.survey_check): over the design ramp, mean (design RL minus surveyed AHD) = 0.59 m (Nov 1999, n = 454) and 0.50 m (Feb 2002, n = 236), residual sd 0.58 m; reading A needs 0.78, B needs 0.00. The highest sounding is -1.40 m (Nov 1999) / -1.67 m (Feb 2002) AHD; the design crest is -1.78 m (A) or -1.00 m (B). Reading A is the nearer, but not exact: the 0.2-0.3 m gap means either rock placed above the design level or AHD about 0.2-0.3 m above MSL (A12). Unresolved (needs the Fremantle chart-datum to AHD offset from Transport WA).
- Water levels (z_MSL): datum -0.78, typical low/high water -0.40 / +0.40 (range 0.8 m, Blacka Table 4.3; symmetry assumed, A7), MSL 0. BoM Fremantle gauge (above gauge zero): MSL 0.773 m (mean of annual means 1992-2011, 20 years; scripts/shore_and_datum.py), highest recorded 1.947 m (16 May 2003), lowest -0.100 m (1 Jan 1987); HAT / LAT not published on the pages found.

### 3.2 Plan and georeference
Scale: four printed-dimension readings on Fig A.2 give 6.57 (26.0 m transect), 6.49 (30.0 m transect), 6.52 (0.5 m contours at 1:20 = 10 m) and 6.46 px/m (40.0 m = RL -3.0 to -1.0 at 1:20): mean 6.51, sd 0.05 (annotated/A1). Outline: silhouette of the drawn structure = outer foot ('interface line'), 111 vertices (../METHOD.md steps 4-5). Extension: 'additional fill area' triangle of img-06 registered on corners P2 and P1 (../METHOD.md step 6).
Georeference (scripts/georef_fit.py): pre-construction bed = linear interpolation (scipy LinearNDInterpolator) of the 1998 'Reef' soundings; a Nov 1999 sounding is 'rock' if it lies more than 0.6 m above that bed (564 soundings) and 'bed' if less than 0.2 m (135). Rotation, easting and northing of the traced original-plan outline are found by differential evolution (scipy) maximising (share of rock soundings inside) - (share of bed soundings inside), scale fixed: theta = 9.74 deg ccw, anchor (centroid vertex mean) at 32.015644 S, 115.748462 E (E 381801.49, N 6457145.86, MGA 50). 91.0 % of rock and 18.5 % of bed soundings are inside the outline. With the scale also free the optimum is 0.979 (the drawing scale is confirmed within 2 %). Independent check: the outline lies on the dark rock patch of four Esri images (3.7).

### 3.3 Design surface (RL)
The drawing shows two hatched rock bands (outer along the apex and arms, inner along the V's shoreward edge) and, between them, contours RL -2.5, -2.0, -1.5 (and -1.0 at the inner edge, -3.0 at the outer one). The region between the bands (the 'ramp', 3,113 m2) is traced as a polygon (dense-hatch mask removed from the silhouette, smoothed by a 1.5 m open-close). With d_out and d_in the distances from a point to the outer (set-out) and inner (crest) edge of the ramp: `z_RL = -3.0 + 2.0 * d_out / (d_out + d_in)` (A10). Check against the drawing: on the centreline the model gives RL -2.59, -2.20, -1.62 where the drawing has -2.5, -2.0, -1.5 (rms 0.14 m); on the 26.0 m and 30.0 m transects the crossings lie within 0.1-1.4 m (annotated/A1). Used for the two design versions only.

### 3.4 Seabed (measured) and rock volume
- Bed (scripts/make_bed.py): the DoT single-beam survey ending 30 Mar 1998 (14,303 soundings in the window, pre-construction) averaged per 1 m cell and interpolated linearly where a sounding is less than 8 m away; elsewhere the DoT Perth_5m composite (bilinear), shifted by its mean difference to the 1998 soundings more than 120 m from the reef (+0.085 m; the composite contains the reef as a smoothed mound, so points on the reef are excluded); the whole field smoothed by a Gaussian of sigma 2 m (removes sounding-line stripes) and resampled into the canonical frame at 1 m (bed_measured_canonical.npz; 52 % of the grid from the survey). At the anchor the bed is -3.68 m AHD; along the original outline 2.84 to 6.63 m below MSL (reading A), mean 4.27 m. The bed 'RL' used inside the design-surface code is AHD + 0.78. Accuracy: 1995 and 1998 single-beam surveys agree to a mean of 0.09 m, sd 0.29 m (nearest soundings within 3 m, n = 8,063); so +-0.3 m (A14).
- Design versions (A5, A6): in the bands (footprint minus ramp) the surface falls linearly from the ramp-edge level to the bed over the drawn band width (3-7 m); the extension fill is pyramid-like, scaled to 320 m3. Volume = design surface minus measured bed over the footprint (0.5 m raster): 3,920 m3 (original), 4,249 m3 (with extension); mean thickness 0.97 / 0.98 m, max 2.30 / 2.47 m. Not fitted to any stated volume (the first model fitted a plane bed to 5,500 m3).
- Surveyed versions (build_3d.py survey_version; A15-A17): thickness at each Nov 1999 (or Feb 2002) sounding inside the footprint + 6 m = sounding z minus the model bed at that point; linear interpolation (LinearNDInterpolator, nearest-neighbour fill outside the hull) onto the 0.5 m raster; negative values set to 0; Gaussian smoothing sigma 1 m inside the footprint; surface = bed + thickness. Footprint = original plan (the soundings cannot resolve the edge to better than a few metres). Volume: 4,671 m3 (Nov 1999), 5,219 m3 (Feb 2002); mean thickness 1.15 / 1.29 m; max 2.76 / 2.69 m; highest point -1.55 / -1.71 m AHD (after smoothing). scripts/survey_volume.py gives the same without smoothing (4,751 / 5,201 m3).
- Seabed around the reef: the same measured field for y = 190-420 m, x = -150..150 m (model frame).
- Rasters: 0.5 m (volumes), exported at 1 m.

### 3.5 Extension test
Soundings of Feb 2002 inside the drawn triangle (outside the original plan): n = 23, mean thickness +0.43 m (median 0.29), against +1.29 m inside the original plan and +0.19 m in the 25 m ring outside the footprint (n = 809). Drawn fill: 0.92 m mean. The Nov 1999 survey has 27 soundings there. On the Esri images the triangle is brighter than the rock interior (ratio 1.1-2.9) and about as bright as the ring. The extension is therefore NOT confirmed as built at the drawn size; it stays as a separate version (X10).

### 3.6 Shoreline, offshore distance and tides (scripts/shore_and_datum.py)
Shoreline = first land cell (>= 0 m AHD) of every 4th row of the Perth_5m clip within +-400 m of the anchor, least-squares line: bearing 7.8 deg T. Anchor to the 0 m AHD line: 254 m (due east), 263 m (080 / 100 deg): between Pattiaratchi (250 m) and Blacka / RWR (275 m). Arm bearings after georeferencing: north arm 35 deg T, south arm 128 deg T; relative to the shoreline 27 and 60 deg (the design peel angle is 45 deg to the wave crest, not to the shoreline; the frame axis is not shore-normal).

### 3.7 Imagery check
Esri tiles (zoom 19) around 32.0155 S 115.7478 E for 14 distinct Wayback imagery versions (197 releases deduplicated by hash on a 3 x 3 tile mosaic at zoom 17) and the current layer. The georeferenced outline (3.2, no image information used in the fit) is drawn on 5 images (../src/imagery/annotated/). The dark rock patch coincides with the outline in all; on 2020-03-23 a wave breaks on the structure. Mean luminance inside the outline (eroded 3 m) against a ring 4-14 m outside: 0.35-0.87 (darker inside) on the 7 images measured; the extension triangle is as bright as the ring or brighter. Wayback capture dates are not available (only release dates), so no image is dated to a month.

## 5. Uncertainty
- Plan: scale confirmed within 2 % (soundings) and +-3 % (drawing) -> area +-4-6 % (4,105-4,629 m2 at the drawing's +-3 %); position +-2 m; edge +-0.5 m. Extension: unconfirmed.
- Surveyed surfaces: vertical +-0.3 m (single-beam, DoT conversion to AHD, bed interpolation); between lines (about 5 m) the surface is interpolated, narrow ridges are smoothed; volume +-1,000 m3 (bed +-0.25 m over 4,050 m2). Negative-thickness clipping biases the volume slightly high.
- Design versions: crest and set-out levels exact as labels; datum link +-0.3 m (survey) to 0.78 m (A against B); volume +-1,200-1,300 m3 from the bed term (+-0.3 m x area).
- Seabed: +-0.3 m under the reef (survey), +-0.5 m beyond the survey coverage (Perth_5m, 5 m cells, composite of unknown dates).
- Vertical exaggeration (1-5x, default 1x): heights only; plan is true; always shown on screen.

## 6. Confidence
MEDIUM overall (first run: LOW). Plan / footprint: HIGH for the original design plan (survey + imagery), LOW for the extension. Seabed under the reef: MEDIUM-HIGH (measured, +-0.3 m, 1998 survey 1 year before construction). Rock surface: MEDIUM (surveyed, single-beam, +-0.3 m vertical, interpolated between lines); design versions: idealised, volume 16-25 % below the surveyed. Datum: MEDIUM (A supported within 0.2-0.3 m; the exact link is unresolved). Tides: LOW-MEDIUM (range and MSL measured at the Fremantle gauge (Corkhill Landing, about 4 km south of the reef); HAT / LAT not found).

## 7. Limitations and unknowns (and what resolves them)
1. Datum link RL / AHD / MSL (0.2-0.3 m): Transport WA (Tides@transport.wa.gov.au) for the Fremantle chart datum to AHD offset and HAT / LAT; Lyons (1992) drawing set.
2. Extension: not confirmed. A survey after 2002, or the Pattiaratchi (2003, 2007) / Bancroft (1999) texts, could say whether and how much was built.
3. Rock surface between sounding lines and after 2002 (settlement, Pitt 2009 'crest about 1.5 m below the lowest tide'): a newer multibeam / lidar survey (DoT WA Bathymetry Portal, AusSeabed) or Navionics readings by Lior at the crest and apex (3d/REQUESTS_FOR_LIOR.md). The DoT composite Perth_5m includes the reef only as a smoothed mound.
4. Imagery capture dates: Wayback gives release dates only; Google Earth Pro historical imagery (Lior) would date them.
5. Papers not available: Bancroft (1999), Ranasinghe et al. (2001), Pattiaratchi (2003, 2007) (02_research/dead_ends/cables-reef-wa.json).
6. Materials: granite (Blacka, RWR) vs limestone (Mead and Borrero 2011): not used in the geometry.
