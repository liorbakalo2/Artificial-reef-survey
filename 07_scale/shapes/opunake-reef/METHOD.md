## 2026-10-13 - TRC Technical Report 2009-33 and NZ Geographic (Lior's uploads P15, P16; agent: Opunake TRC/NZGeo task)
Scripts: scripts/build_consent_georef.py (all numbers -> consent_georef.json, Fig 1 sidecar, overlays), scripts/update_shape_json_2026-10-13.py (shape.json). Citations and page map: sources.md "New sources 2026-10-13".
1. Read both PDFs with pymupdf. TRC: text layer on PDF pp. 1-27 and 45-58; Appendix I (the three consents, PDF pp. 28-42) is scanned: OCRed with tesseract 5.3.4 at 300 dpi (05_qa/papers_intake/trc_2009-33_opunake_appendixI_consents_ocr.md). NZ Geographic print has no text layer: OCRed at 200 dpi (05_qa/papers_intake/nzgeo_moores_2006_artificial_surf_reefs_ocr.md). The NZ Geographic article has NO Opunake figure (text only; one unlabelled hero photograph).
2. TRC printed p. 3-4 (PDF 9-10) gives the design: reef built in 3.0 to 1.8 m water depth below chart datum, shallowest contour 0.0 m below chart datum; area about 2,970 m2, volume about 4,900 m3, about 30 m wide, 3.5 m high, 99 m long; 27 bags in place as of April 2008, fine-tuning planned for summer 2009/2010. 99 x 30 = 2,970 exactly, so the area is a bounding rectangle (A10). Chart datum = lowest astronomical tide (glossary, printed p. 19).
3. TRC Figure 1 (printed p. 3): aerial with a white rectangle 'Proposed location of surf reef' and an orange rectangle 'Area of water to be used during the construction process'. No reef plan, no bags drawn.
4. Consent 6376-1 (Appendix I, PDF p. 39): 'to occupy an area of the coastal marine area within Opunake Bay defined by the Taranaki local circuit grid co-ordinates 257484E-663800N, 267650E-663887N, 267696E-663803N, 267525E-663718N ... GR: P20:832-937'. Checked on the page image (crop saved): the first easting is printed 257484.
5. Misprint: with 257484E the corner is 10 km west of the other three; with 267484E the four corners close as a rectangle (angles 91.1, 91.0, 87.7, 90.1 deg; sides 187.4, 95.8, 191.0, 91.7 m) and match the GR. Used 267484E (A5).
6. Projection: 'Taranaki local circuit grid' = NZGD49 / Taranaki Circuit (EPSG:27209; FE 300000, FN 700000: checked, Opunake falls at E 267 500 N 663 800). Converted to NZGD2000/WGS84 with pyproj 3.8.0 and the LINZ grid nz_linz_nzgd2kgrid0005.tif (0.2 m); the PROJ 7-parameter fallback differs by 3.7 m (kept as the uncertainty bound). GR check: corner A in NZMG (EPSG:27200, same datum) = 2583212 E 6193647 N = map reference 832-936 on NZMS 260 P20; the consent GR 832-937 is a 100 m reference: consistent (A6).
7. Result: consent area 189 x 94 m (mean sides 189.2 / 93.7), area 17,726 m2, long axis grid bearing 63.0 (true azimuth 63.2), centre lat -39.459433 lon 173.851625 (NZMG 2583316 E 6193648 N). The card's old lat/lon (-39.46, 173.86) is 720 m east of this centre.
8. Georeference of Fig 1: read the four corners of the white and orange rectangles on 10x grids of the embedded 417 x 273 px image (+-0.5 px = +-2.2 m); 4-GCP affine (white corners <-> consent corners): residuals 0.3 m (a parallelogram fits exactly, so this checks the readings only), 4.55 m/px along x, 4.42 m/px along y, rotation 0.6 deg (north-up). The white rectangle is therefore the CONSENT AREA (189 x 94 m), not the 99 x 30 m reef. Independent checks that do not use the GCPs: (a) boat-ramp hardstanding picked by eye in Fig 1 and in an Esri 2025 frame: predicted vs picked position 0.7 m apart (pick error 5-10 m); (b) overlay of the warped Fig 1 on Esri 2025: coast, headland and groyne line up (overlays/fig1_georef_check_on_esri2025.png); (c) the scale bar: tick spacing 1:1:2 and 3-character labels at ticks 3-4 -> read 0/75/150/300 m = 4.64 m/px, 3.5 percent from the consent-derived value (labels illegible: A9). Orange rectangle: about 320 x 200 m, circuit corners in consent_georef.json (not a consent boundary in the text we hold).
9. First attempt (rejected): assuming the orange rectangle was the consent area gave 2.56 x 2.16 m/px (anisotropic) and put the boat ramp 115 m from its true position; the white rectangle fits (isotropic, ramp 0.7 m). Recorded because it explains why the figure's labels are easy to misread.
10. TRC Fig 2 (printed p. 10): hatched 'Approximate location' rectangle has the same 2:1 shape but 15 deg above horizontal vs 27-28 deg in Fig 1 / the coordinates: hand-drawn schematic, not used.
11. Assumed envelope: 99 x 30 m rectangle centred on the consent centre, long axis parallel to the consent long axis (A7, A8). 99 m cannot lie across the 94 m side, so the axis is within +-47 deg of the long side; margins give +-47 m along and +-32 m across the axis for the position. Blacka et al. 2013 (30 m longshore x 100 m cross-shore, depth 1.8-3.0 m at LAT) is the same design as TRC (99 x 30, 1.8-3.0 m below chart datum), and the long axis being cross-shore to Opunake Beach fits the consent axis (the headland shore NW of the polygon also runs at about 63 deg).
12. Size conflict 90 x 20 m vs 99 x 30 m: the Daily News of 11 June 2004 (Stuff copy, fetched with curl) quotes the Opunake Artificial Reef Committee chairman: bags 1.5-2 m high covering 'around 90m long and 20m wide', slight hook, NZ$1.1 m (council decision): the 2004 concept, which Newsroom 2021, Raised Water Research and SurferToday repeat. The consent-stage design (2005-06, TRC 2009) is 99 x 30 x 3.5 m = Blacka 100 x 30 m. Resolved in favour of TRC (D8).
13. Imagery check: consent polygon and envelope drawn on LINZ 2011-12 (0.4 m, EPSG:2193): only wave texture inside the area; the bags (crest at chart datum) are not visible (overlays/consent_area_and_envelope_on_linz2011-12.png). Consistent with the earlier negative findings (A2).
14. Not found: any drawing of the bag layout, per-bag levels, post-construction survey (TRC only recommends a dive survey for 2009-10, printed p. 18), construction manual (Black and Mead 2006, cited in TRC bibliography, printed p. 20), detailed design plan (consent condition 3) and the as-built position map +-5 m (condition 10, to be sent to TRC, LINZ Hydrographic Office, RNZ Navy, Maritime Safety Authority).
