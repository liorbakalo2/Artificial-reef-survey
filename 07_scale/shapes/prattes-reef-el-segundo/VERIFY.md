## Check 2 - design version (run 3, 2026-10-05)
- Sources re-opened: RWR page (fresh download; img1 md5 7db4b8ab... identical to src\), Wikipedia 'Chevron Reef', ICCE-2010 PDF (text extracted), CCC staff report W5a-10-1998 (text extracted), Coastal Frontiers removal page, Surfrider news, Beachapedia, and the ResearchGate page of Borrero & Nelsen (2003) in the browser pane.
- What was built: Phase I = 110 bags, Sept/fall 2000 (ICCE 2010, Wikipedia, Coastal Frontiers). - What the drawing is: the Skelly Engineering plan with BAG COUNT 110 and OPTIONAL BAGS 30. Borrero & Nelsen (2003) Fig. 4 'The design layout of Pratte's Reef' (viewed at 640 px in the browser pane) is this same drawing, with the 0 / 15 / 30 / 60 / 90 ft bar complete. Henriquez 2005 Fig. 2.10 is the same figure again. Every publication calls it the DESIGN layout. I found no as-built plan, no post-Phase-II plan, and no earlier/later redesign other than the 1996-98 concept (img6, about 30 large bags, two 150 ft wings at about 90 deg, not to scale).
- Verdict: the drawing represents the PHASE I DESIGN LAYOUT of fall 2000 with solid-outline bags only (30 'optional' bags excluded). It is NOT the as-built (bags were crane-placed, moved, sank and were buried: RWR, Borrero 2010) and NOT the 200-bag reef after April 2001. The page must say so. Phase I remains the base of the final structure (Phase II was stacked on it), so the footprint is a fair lower bound for the plan extent, with the crest wider than drawn after Phase II.
- Side finding (datum): the drawing's 'TOP OF BAG MIN DEPTH -6' MSL' is 0.97 m below MLLW (NOAA LA 9410660: MSL = MLLW + 2.82 ft), close to the post-Phase-II crest (-0.9 m MLLW) and 0.8 m shallower than ICCE's as-installed Phase I outermost depth (1.8 m below MLLW). The depth label cannot decide which phase is drawn; the bag count does.
