## 3D inputs check (done 2026-10-06) - each METHOD.md "Inputs for 3D" value re-checked against its cited page

- Caution B (NEW): LINZ standard-port table for Tauranga (re-fetched; 1 Jul 2026 - 30 Jun 2027) lists MSL = 1.13 m above CD, i.e. PRESENT mean sea level is 0.17 m above MVD-53 (NIWA 2006 Table 2: MLOS +0.069 m for 1987-2005, +0.090 m for 2002-2005, rising).
