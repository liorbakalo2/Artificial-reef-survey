## What this changes for the model (nothing is changed; listed for main)
1. **The EMODnet offshore gradient (-1.1 %, soundings y 368-652 m, A7, E4; validation lines "EMODnet cells") comes from a survey of March-May 1969**, 43 years before the reef (completed 8 March 2012) and 57 years before today, by single-beam echosounder, with one sounding per DTM cell. METHODS_3D, SOURCES_3D (row "emodnet") and the image registry do not state the survey year. The gradient is a shoreface trend of 400-650 m off the defence line and is applied as a slope only, so the model value is not shown to be wrong, but the documented basis is "a 1969 single-beam line survey of a mobile sand shoreface, reduction datum unknown", not "EMODnet survey" in general.
2. **Absolute EMODnet levels at Borth** (validation: model 0.7-1.2 m deeper than the EMODnet cells at y 368-513; E3 `z_MSL = e_LAT - 2.75`): the cells are 1969 soundings reduced by UKHO to a datum that EMODnet labels LAT. The 1969 reduction datum is unknown (older UK chart datums were not always LAT; not verified). The 0.7-1.2 m offset can therefore include a datum shift and 57 years of seabed change, as well as the Navionics and design differences already listed. No value is changed; the offset is not an argument for or against the LAT = -2.44 mODN assumption (A3).
3. **METHODS_3D section 7 limitation "a sounding line or multibeam along y 335-520 m"** stays open. A modern source exists offshore (the MCA/UKHO surveys of Cardigan Bay, e.g. BGS lists HI1450 "Approaches to Cardigan Bay, 28/07/2014 to 25/11/2014", found by web search, not opened) but it is a different survey and not the EMODnet source.
