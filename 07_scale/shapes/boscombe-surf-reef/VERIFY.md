## Check 7 - confidence level and reason. Ruling: HIGH -> MEDIUM
Question put to me: the tracer rated 'high' although the text area (~1 ha) is 2.5x the traced 4,042 m2 bag field, arguing that the text areas contradict each other while the designers' plan and an independent 2011 survey agree with the trace.

Rubric (SHAPE_SPEC.md): 'high: structure clearly visible on geo-referenced imagery or an as-built survey with a scale, AND dimensions agree with text within ~10%.' 'medium: traced on a scaled design drawing, or structure only partly visible, or dimensions differ 10-25% from text.' 'low: no image of the structure.'

Reasoning:
1. First clause of 'high' is met twice over (Esri 2011 geo-referenced imagery with the bags resolvable; the April 2011 DGPS survey plan with gridlines).
2. Second clause is NOT met for the headline dimension. 4,042 m2 vs ~10,000 m2 is -60%. Several other dimensions do agree (distance east of the pier +0.7%, shore normal +0.2%, offshore distance +9.6% from the seawall base), but the footprint area is the number the page and the card put first, and the text for it is not a single stray value: Wikipedia, Raised Water Research and a peer-reviewed 2017 paper (Herbert et al., PLoS ONE) all say about 1 ha. The tracer's 'the text areas contradict each other' is true only for the outliers (45,000 m2, 'football pitch', a 50 x 70 m snippet); the 1 ha cluster is mutually consistent, even if probably copied from one origin.
3. The rubric has no band for >25% disagreement. Medium is the nearest level below high and the one whose logic ('dimensions disagree with text') applies; low is reserved for 'no image of the structure', which is plainly not the case. The brief says to lower confidence rather than guess.
4. Evidence that the DRAWING (not the text) is right is strong and is stated in the reason, but it is inference, not a source that defines the 1 ha: 13,000 m3 of sand = 3.2 m mean thickness on 4,042 m2 (fits two container layers, 4.5-5.5 m relief) vs 1.3 m on 1 ha; the designers' model holds 11,540 m3 in 5,255 m2 (relief >0.5 m); three plan-view sources give 3,400-5,300 m2. A shore-aligned 88 x 94 m bounding box = 0.83 ha shows how a 'roughly 1 ha' envelope could arise, which is a hypothesis only.
5. Therefore 'medium' is the honest label, with a reason that tells the reader the outline is well supported and the discrepancy is with the text. The tracer anticipated this in METHOD.md step 5 ('the orchestrator may prefer medium if it reads the rubric literally'); I read it literally.

Reason now in shape.json confidence.reason (shown on the page): 'The bag field is clearly visible on a geo-referenced 2011 satellite image and its outline matches an April 2011 survey and the designers' own plan, so shape and position are well supported. But its area (0.40 ha) is only 40% of the roughly 1 hectare quoted by Wikipedia, Raised Water Research and a 2017 journal paper, and the rating scale asks for agreement within about 10%, so it is rated medium; the 13,000 m3 of sand fits 0.4-0.5 ha, which suggests the 1 ha figure is a loose envelope, but no source defines it.'
