### Datum of the Fig 9 survey (the weakest link) - reasoning, 2026-10-05
- Rendle 2012 p.8 says "In the UK, mean sea level (MSL) is equivalent to Chart Datum, Newlyn ... (0 m contour in Fig. 10)" - garbled (chart datum is NOT MSL); it concerns the beach 0 m contour, and could mean Ordnance Datum Newlyn (CCO beach surveys use ODN).
- Evidence the reef survey depth axis is Chart Datum: (1) the 2009 crest in Fig 9 is +0.4 .. +0.8 and the designers' crest is +0.5 m ACD (a 1.4 m ODN offset would put the crest 1.4 m above the design); (2) Rendle p.3 "225 m offshore in 2.7 to 5 m depth" and Mead "water depths of 3-5 m (CD)" match the survey seabed at the reef (-3 .. -5); (3) Herbert 2017: upper section exposed on low SPRING tides = crest +0.5..+0.8 ACD, not +1.9 ACD; (4) EMODnet (LAT) is deeper than the survey by about 1.5 m, not 3 m (check below).
- Decision: survey datum = Chart Datum (ACD). The viewer keeps an "alternative datum" switch (survey zero = ODN ~ MSL) so the effect of being wrong can be seen. Flagged in provenance as estimated, uncertainty +-1.4 m (all absolute heights; relief unaffected).

### EMODnet coarse check (WMS GetFeatureInfo, layer emodnet:mean, retrieved 2026-10-05)
- https://ows.emodnet-bathymetry.eu/wms (GetFeatureInfo). Cells ~115 m, values in m relative to LAT. Along the shore normal through the reef (canonical x = 0): y 100-200: -3.99 m; y 220-320: -5.62 m; y 340-440: -7.05 m; beach y -20..80: +0.14 m. Survey (April 2011, as ACD): y 100-200 about -1.4..-2.5; y 220-300 about -3 .. -4.8. EMODnet is deeper by 1.3-1.7 m (older / smoothed, pre-nourishment data probable), which is closer to the CD reading than to ODN (3 m). Only used as a datum plausibility check, not for values.

### Registration of the survey grid to the satellite outline (computed 2026-10-05)
- Survey Z (colour read, converted OSGB -> canonical via the img1 georeference, scripts/canon.py) minus an ambient order-2 polynomial seabed = relief. Relief mask > 1.0 m covers 4,078 m2 vs the satellite outline 4,042 m2; IoU 0.90 with NO shift (best shift search +-30 m: 0, 0). So the survey grid is used UNSHIFTED. (The earlier 0.77 IoU / 8.5 m centroid offset in shape.json came from tracing the -2/-3 m colour edge, not from a misregistration.)
