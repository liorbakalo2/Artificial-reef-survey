## 3 - Trace and rectification (all steps reproducible with rectify_fit.py)
3.1 Photo-space trace: 18 vertices read with overlay.py zoom grids (20 px), file pixel_polys_img1_v2.json, overlay overlays/img1_trace_v2.png. The polygon is the outer edge of the visible strips plus the whitewater patch (whitewater sits on the shore-ward end of the structure). Uncertainty: +-8 px in the clear part, +-25 px at the left tip (strips fade into haze).
3.2 Why a camera fit: the photo has no scale, no georeference and no surveyed point on the water. Single-view method: pinhole camera, zero roll, principal point at the photo centre (467, 360), horizon row 191 gives the pitch for any focal length f (tan pitch = (360-191)/f). Unknowns: camera east, north, height H, azimuth, f.
3.3 Ground control: the swash line (landward edge of the surf foam) read at 14 points on the photo (overlays/img1_camera_fit_check.png, yellow) and 11 points on the 2011-02-28 Esri frame (georeferenced by its .geo.json; native 1.18 m/px; points read on a 2x view, +-10 view px = +-6 m). Fit = nearest distance (px) from each photo point to the satellite curve projected through the camera.
3.4 Search: 250 random starts with H free (25-120 m): one basin, H = 34 m, azimuth 326 deg, f = 1,045 px (46 deg lens), rms 2.2 px; camera at 8.38724 N, 76.97608 E, i.e. at the tombolo rock (consistent with the foreground rocks in the photo). Profile over H: rms 2.2 (H 35), 2.5-2.8 (H 40-80), 3.7 (90), 6.4 (100), 15.8 (130). **The photo therefore does not fix the scale**: the footprint is 546 m2 at H 30 and 1,681 m2 at H 80 (rectify_result.json, H_sweep).
3.5 Choice of H: f falls as H rises (1,190 px at 30 m; 612 px = 72 deg at 60 m; 427 px = 93 deg at 80 m). A normal compact camera is 55-75 deg, so H 40-65 m is plausible. H = 60 m reproduces the 90 m offshore distance of WRL Table 4.2 with a 72 deg lens (24 mm-equivalent) and is the default; H = 35 m (best fit) and H = 80 m (best match to WRL's 65 m long-shore size and 90 m offshore) are kept as the other two versions. This choice uses a secondary-source number, so the offshore agreement is not an independent check (said in shape.json).
3.6 Unproject: each traced pixel is intersected with the sea plane (z = 0): ground = camera + t * ray, t = H / (-ray_z). Local metres (E, N) about 8.3885 N, 76.9745 E (111,320*cos(lat) m/deg east, 110,574 m/deg north), converted to lat/lon for shape.json geo.
3.7 Canonical frame: origin = nearest point of the 2011 swash line to the reef centroid (8.387161 N, 76.975992 E); +x along the swash line (bearing 196 deg, SSW), +y offshore (bearing 286 deg, WNW). No use of geom.py make-canonical (avoids the old rotation bug); rotation done explicitly: x = d.t, y = d.n.
3.8 Result (default, H 60 m): area 1,216 m2, 59 m along x, 41 m across y, 65-106 m from the 2011 swash line (mean 90 m), strips at about 38 deg to the swash line (edge bearings 231 and 238 deg vs swash 196 deg, +-15 deg). Position: immediately west/north-west of the tombolo rock (edge of the outline within about 0-30 m of the rock), at the Lighthouse (south) end of the bay. Positional uncertainty +-20 m (swash points +-6 m, swash line 2010 vs 2011, horizon +-6 px: area 1,177-1,251 m2).

## 4 - Design version
Only one version was built (Feb 2010). The 2005 Kerala Tourism proposal (Eve's Beach, 500 m x 200 m offshore) was never built. The aerial shows the intact reef. The reef later deflated and sank (Raised Water Research, card; WRL p.70 citing Rendle pers. comm. 2013: damage hampers protection); that state is not drawn. Volume: 4,000 m3 (Mead & Borrero 2011; WRL cost rate gives ~4,017 m3) vs 4,800 m3 in the card (ASR via RWR) - unresolved.

## 5 - Confidence: LOW
See shape.json. Reasons: one oblique unscaled photo; scale free (H); swash line from a different date; no survey. What would raise it: a vertical image of the reef (Google Earth historical 2010-2012, Esri Wayback z19 of the 2011-02-28 capture) or an ASR/CESS plan.
