## 5. Consistency notes
- 2026-10-13: the City gives "140 m offshore" (design table, project page, news 7 Mar 2025) and "120 m offshore" (news 16 Jul 2025, RWR); the model uses 140 m from the landward toe as drawn. Funding: City page lists Commonwealth 4.75 M and WA 5 M; the WA Government statement (S21) and RWR give WA 4.75 M (card to flag).
- Footprint 13,104 m2 / 10,918 m2 and the crest and tonnage numbers are the same in shape.json, model.js and this file. The City FAQ (2023) gives an earlier 16,000 m2 footprint and 145 m offshore; the final table gives 140 m (shape.json decision).
- Open mismatches: BMT near-shore soundings vs RHDHV contours (Methods 4).
