## 2. Assumptions
| id | assumed | why / evidence | effect | what would replace it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | the built reef has the footprint of the final design (default) | City dimensions equal the design table; satellite wedge matches (IoU 0.84); no as-built survey | plan: -30 %/+20 % by edge | practical-completion survey |
| A2 | AHD = MSL; LAT = AHD - 0.74 m (mean of 2024 predicted extremes 0.742 m above LAT) | AHD is defined about MSL; no Albany tidal-plane table found | +-0.1 m on the tide labels; none on geometry | BoM/DoT tidal planes with AHD |
| A3 | contour depths of the RHDHV 2015 plan are metres below AHD | legend crest -0.75 m matches the report's "-0.75 mAHD" (S10); datum not printed on the plan | if CD = LAT: bed 0.74 m deeper = +5.7k m3 (+17 %) | the original RHDHV report figure |
| A4 | seabed uniform alongshore (one profile for all x) | contours parallel within +-20 % spacing over x -160 ... 0; x > 0 contaminated by the concept structure | local +-1 m | measured bathymetry |
| A5 | beach (+1 m at y = -30, +2 m at -60) and seaward nodes (-7.5 m at 250 m, -8.0 at 330, -8.3 at 420) | -8 contour and BMT 8.4 m CD sounding; beach not read | outside the reef; offshore toe +-0.5 m | survey |
| A6 | crest ridge 9 m inside the shoreward edge | design plan contours dense along that edge | 5 m / 14 m offset = -1.2k / +1.2k m3 | design drawing |
| A7 | ridge level -1.0 m AHD over the first 15 m from the OFFSHORE end, ramp to -2.0 m at 40 m, -2.0 m beyond | table gives -1.00 m "shallowest" and "110 m @ -2 m"; the end of the peak is not stated (take-off area is at the east end in the design plan) | all -1.0 m: +6.4k m3; all -2.0 m: -1.4k m3 | section drawing |
| A8 | surface = loft between ridge and toe, no toe slope steeper than 1:2 | no source gives slopes; 1:2 is the usual steepest rock slope (Borth design 1:1.5) | shape of slopes; the limit lowered the unfitted volume by 1 % | design cross-sections |
| A9 | loft exponent N = 2.73, fitted to 34,000 m3 for the DESIGN outline | the City volume is the only quantity; unfitted (N = 1) the loft gives 20,826 m3 (61 %) | the volume of the design outline is NOT a check; the satellite version (30,273 m3) is a result | an independent volume or section |
| A10 | rock density 1.847 t/m3 | 62,800 t / 34,000 m3 (both City figures) | tonnes follow volume | - |
| A11 | one rock surface (belly/core/armour not modelled separately) | no layer geometry published | none on the outer shape | - |
| A12 | state = as built March 2025 | 18 rocks moved March 2026 (Albany Advertiser, 11 Mar 2026) | note only | - |

## 4. Confidence per element
plan HIGH; crest level MEDIUM-LOW; seabed LOW; pile height and slopes LOW; volume MEDIUM-LOW (design outline fitted); tides LOW-MEDIUM; overall LOW. Reasons: `METHODS_3D.md` section 6.

## 5. Consistency notes
- Footprint 13,104 m2 / 10,918 m2 and the crest and tonnage numbers are the same in shape.json, model.js and this file. The City FAQ (2023) gives an earlier 16,000 m2 footprint and 145 m offshore; the final table gives 140 m (shape.json decision).
- Open mismatches: BMT near-shore soundings vs RHDHV contours (Methods 4).
