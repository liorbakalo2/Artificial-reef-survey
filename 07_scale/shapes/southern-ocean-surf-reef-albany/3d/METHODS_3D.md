# METHODS_3D - Southern Ocean Surf Reef ("Midds Reef"), Middleton Beach, Albany WA

Written 2026-10-10 (3D agent, fresh run with network access). Updated 2026-10-13 (Lior's four uploads and the live pages read; geometry unchanged, dates and notes corrected, section 9 gives before/after). Model files: `model.js`, `docs.js`, `index.html`; regenerate with `python -I build_3d.py`. Source register, numbered assumptions and decisions: `REGISTER_3D.md` (also in the viewer, Methods tab). Run log: `SOURCES_3D.md`.

## 1. Summary
The model shows the rock reef as completed in mid-2025 (placement 18 March - June 2025, opened 16 July 2025; the rock movements of May 2025 and February 2026 are notes only, section 3.4). The **plan** (toe outline) is the Bluecoast final-design outer contour, 13,104 m2, positioned on the satellite image; the toggle is the dark rock patch visible on the Esri image of 2026-01-20, 10,918 m2. The **crest** levels are the City of Albany design values (-1.0 m AHD shallowest, 110 m of crest at -2 m AHD). The **seabed** is a single shore-normal profile read from contour lines of a 2015 Royal HaskoningDHV design plan (depth datum not stated, taken as AHD), so the pile height (up to 6.0 m, mean 2.6 m over the design footprint) is only known to about +-1 m. The reef surface is a loft between a crest ridge and the toe outline; its fullness (exponent N = 2.73) is **fitted** so that the design outline holds the stated 34,000 m3, so that volume is not a check. **3D confidence: LOW** (plan high, crest medium-low, seabed low, tides low-medium).

## 3. Methods
### 3.1 Frame and datum
Canonical frame of `shape.json`: origin = foot of the perpendicular from the reef centroid onto the beach-face line (-35.0172584, 117.919428); +x alongshore toward NNE (bearing 30.26 deg true), +y offshore toward ESE (120.26 deg); z up, **z = 0 at AHD, taken as MSL (A2)**. LAT is at z = -0.74 m: the mean of the 1,043 predicted high and low waters of 2024 (WA DoT/BoM, datum LAT) is 0.742 m above LAT (`scripts/tides_from_dot.py`, `src/tides_derived.json`), and AHD is assumed equal to MSL. Equations: depth below LAT = -z + 0.74; z_AHD(bed) = -(depth below CD) - 0.74 if the chart datum is LAT. MLLW = -0.26 and MHHW = +0.34 m AHD are the City's own statement ("crest -1.0 m AHD is 0.74 m below MLLW and 1.34 m below MHHW"). The slider top is the highest predicted tide of 2024 (+0.66 m AHD); true HAT was not found.

### 3.2 Seabed
1. The RHDHV Option B plan (`src/rwr_images/..._plan_2015.jpg`, 609 x 354 px, a Google Earth view with coloured depth contours -0.5 ... -8 m) was georeferenced onto an Esri 2025-01-16 mosaic (`3d/src/esri_wayback13192_2025-01-16_z18_wide1200m.png`, 0.489 m/px) with 4 control points (the Surfers car park, two dune tracks, the caravan-park buildings): similarity transform, scale 2.56 m per plan pixel, rotation 0.1 deg, residuals 6.6-12.4 px of the Esri image (3.2-6.1 m) (`scripts/geo.py`). A check: the beach-face line and the Bluecoast outline fall where they should on this plan (annotated A1).
2. Along 8 shore-normal transects (x = -160 ... 0, 20 m apart) the image was sampled every 0.25 m; contour lines were detected as colour peaks against the local median water colour and assigned to depth levels by colour (hue) in order from the black -1 m line (`scripts/prof.py`, `scripts/read_contours.py`, raw crossings in `src/levels2_raw.json`). The level colours were taken from the plan legend. Transects at x > 0 were not used because they run into the contours of the Option B structure drawn on that plan.
3. Medians over the transects give the profile nodes (y from the beach-face line): -1 m at 36, -1.5 at 50, -2 at 64, -2.5 at 75, -3 at 88, -3.5 at 98, -4 at 105 m (n >= 5 transects); -4.5, -5, -6, -7 m at 118, 126, 150, 178 m (n = 2-8; set by eye from the colour sequence, +-10 m). Spacing between lines varies along the shore by about +-20 %.
4. Beyond the nodes: +1.0 m at y = -30 and +2.0 at -60 (beach, ASSUMED A5), -0.5 m at y = 7 (the -0.5 contour), -7.5 at 250, -8.0 at 330, -8.3 at 420 m (the -8 contour of the plan; BMT sounding 8.4 m CD at y = 315 agrees). The profile is **linear interpolated and alongshore uniform** (A4): the seabed grid is 5 m, x -260 ... 260, y -60 ... 420.
5. Not used: the BMT 5 m line and spot soundings near the shore (see 4), EIA appendix Fig 29 (regional model bathymetry in 1 m colour bins, about 10 m pixels, registered img-15 for the record).

### 3.3 Reef surface
1. Toe polygon = the outline of the version (shape.json). The toe sits on the seabed.
2. Crest ridge = the polyline of the first 8 (design) or 6 (satellite) outline vertices along the steep shoreward (+x) edge, offset 9 m into the footprint (A6). Ridge level z_c: -1.0 m AHD over the first 15 m from the offshore (east) end, a linear rise to -2.0 m at 40 m, -2.0 m beyond (A7: the City gives -1.0 m "shallowest" and "110 m at -2 m" but not where).
3. Surface for each 1 m cell inside the outline: z = z_c + (z_b - z_c) * (d_c / (d_c + d_t))^N, with d_c the distance to the ridge, d_t the distance to the toe, z_b the seabed (lofting between crest and toe; no side-slope rule is given by any source, A8); a toe-slope limit (height above the bed <= 0.5 x distance to the toe line, i.e. no slope steeper than 1:2, A8), then a 1.2 m Gaussian smoothing inside the outline.
4. N is found by bisection so that the design outline holds 34,000 m3 (A9): **N = 2.73**. The satellite version uses the same N, ridge rule and seabed, so its volume (30,273 m3) is a result.
5. Resulting slopes: median 1:16, 90th percentile 1:4.5, steepest 1:2.0 (the limit). The design text says "mild seaward slopes and a broad 3D shape"; the design plan shows dense contours along the shoreward edge and wide spacing toward the SW.
6. Rock mass = volume x 1.847 t/m3 (62,800 t / 34,000 m3, A10).

### 3.4 Later changes (notes, not as-built geometry; project rule 5)
- 18 March 2025: first rock placed (Albany Advertiser 18 Mar 2025, S18). Planned 6 weeks (City news 7 Mar 2025, S20); on 16 May 2025 the City said the reef was "more than two-thirds complete", finish "mid-June" (S18); RWR (S17) said "more than 60 % complete" on 28 May 2025. Opened 16 July 2025 (City news S20; ABC S22); the Albany Advertiser (4 Aug 2026, S19) says "after four months of work". The earlier model text "completed March 2025" came from the City programme (practical completion March 2025, S6) and was a plan, not a fact: corrected.
- Monday 26 May 2025 (record swell; highest waves in King George Sound since 2017 per the City, S18): the partly built reef was suspected to have moved; ABC Great Southern photographed rocks that had moved shoreward and surfaced, and Albany Boardriders said the moved rock was the smaller CORE rock, to be covered by armour (S17). A contractor survey of the partly built reef was scheduled (S18). No coordinates, counts or levels are published. Effect on the model: none (the later armour placement and the completion survey are not available).
- Late February 2026 (two days, weather dependent): the City had about 18 "high rocks" repositioned with commercial divers and a local vessel, with Albany Boardriders, after late-2025 surveys flagged problem rocks and surfers reported injuries at low tide; cost "more than $20,000" in the lede and "about $24,000" in the City's estimate (S19, text read from the page metadata, see D12). Which rocks and where are not stated; the article says "the higher rocks" and "those high rocks that would occasionally stick up". Effect on the model: the satellite image of 2026-01-20 predates the move; heights of individual rocks are not modelled; note only.

## 5. Uncertainty
Height of pile above bed h = z_c - z_b: u(h) = sqrt(u_c^2 + u_b^2) with u_c about 0.3 m (design tolerance, not stated) and u_b about 1 m (profile +-0.25 m for position, +-0.74 m datum, up to 2 m near the apex if BMT is right): u(h) about 1.0 m, rising to 2+ m at the shoreward apex. Volume: +-1 m bed = +7.7k / -7.9k m3 (+-23 %) on the design outline (+6.2k / -6.5k on the satellite one); CD = LAT instead of AHD = +5.7k m3; crest all at -1.0 m = +6.4k, all at -2.0 m = -1.4k; ridge offset 5 or 14 m = -1.2k / +1.2k (all with N fixed). Vertical exaggeration (x1 ... x5) changes only the displayed heights; plan and the water level are true. At VE = 5 the 1:21 bed between the -2 m and -5 m contours looks like 1:4.1 and the 6.0 m high pile looks 30 m high.

## 7. Limitations and what would resolve them
- No as-built or bathymetric survey, no section drawing: the Bluecoast Detailed Design Report (full, with the 2017 bathymetry) and the completion survey (mid-2025; the City programme said March 2025) would fix seabed, heights and slopes. Not on the City website (only the executive summary, 5 pages). Lior: request from the City of Albany (e-mail not sent by us).
- Navionics / Garmin Marine Maps did not render in headless Chrome (no map tiles; no login or CAPTCHA was involved, nothing bypassed). Lior can read depths at the points in `REQUESTS_FOR_LIOR.md`; they would test the profile and the datum.
- AusSeabed / Geoscience Australia services (services.ga.gov.au, ausseabed.gov.au) return 403 to this environment and the portal's scripts are served as HTML, so the portal cannot be drawn here (not bypassed). GA's public 2021 holdings file (S23, 42 MB) lists WA Department of Transport surveys of "Middleton Bay & Oyster Harbour" (multibeam, laser scanner) and "Middleton Bay" (single beam) with Data Access URL https://maps.slip.wa.gov.au/Marine/app/: a lead for a measured seabed (request W8, `W8_how_to.png`). Year, resolution and access are NOT known.
- The 2015 contours predate construction by 10 years; sand-bar movement of 1-2 m is plausible.
- Rock grading (belly/core/armour) and the real armour slopes are not modelled.
