## Step 2a - contour-line analysis of s7 (Bluecoast/Nearmap aerial) - what the 'unlabelled contours' tell (2026-10-05)
Method (scripts kept in scratch; reproduced in build_3d.py): oriented line filter (31 px line minus +/-4.5 px flanks, 30 orientations) on the red channel -> ray casting from the slot centroid at 0.5 deg steps -> peaks sorted outward = contour rank k (k=1 is the first line outside the slot).
Findings: (1) lines are nested closed loops with rank continuity all round (checked on overlay); ranks 1-9 close all round, ranks 10-12 exist only on the seaward (E/NE/SE) flank where the outermost line (the toe outline) lies further down. (2) Spacing between lines is constant on each flank: ~52 px (6.1 m) on the N and the outer E flank, ~22 px (2.6 m) on the S flank and W end, ~105 px (12.3 m) between the slot and rank 3 at the E end. (3) If the interval is 0.5 m these are slopes 1:12.2 (N, E), 1:5.2 (S, W), 1:24.5 (E platform near the crest) - which reproduce the DESIGN slopes 1:12 and 1:5 printed on the concept figure (Mortensen 2015 Fig 3: 1/12, 1/5) and the 'curved shoaling platform' text. With a 1.0 m interval the slopes would be 1:6 and 1:2.6, which match nothing in the sources => interval 0.5 m adopted (assumption A-contour). (4) Absolute-elevation reading: slot = crest -1.5 m, rank k = -1.5 - 0.5 k m; the toe outline then sits at about -4.0 m (W end), -5.0 (S), -6.2 (N) and -7.5 m (E end) = seabed deepening seaward by ~3.5 m over ~115 m (about 1:33), the same order as the 1:42-1:50 slope implied by the concept anchors (11.4 m at 560 m offshore).
