No datum stated on the data.gov.au page (must come from the GDB spatial reference / City metadata; AHD is expected).

Raster extent EPSG:28356 (MGA94 zone 56, srid 18 in the gdb): x 511682.5-559879.5, y 6872141.5-6942182.5.
