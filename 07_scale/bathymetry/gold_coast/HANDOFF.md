## 3. Key numbers (units, datum, source id)
- DTM: City 128 m tile grid, MGA56. Palm Beach centre (-28.107334, 153.470913) = E546256 N6890818, block row 370 / col 238 absent (last data block col 235, 273 m landward). Narrowneck centre (-27.9866, 153.4346), block row 265 / col 210 absent (last col 207, 337 m landward). Contour service gives no seabed either (REPORT 2.4).
- Datum: AHD above LAT 0.760 m at the Seaway (MSQ 2014); MSL = LAT + 0.88 (MSQ 2026, 2010-2029); MSL is 0.12 m above AHD; ICM/Jackson 2007 uses a rounded h_AHD = h_LAT - 1.00 (0.24 m more).
- Palm Beach crest: -1.5 m AHD (concept, Mortensen et al. 2015 p.4, Fig 3) / "1.5 m below mean sea level" (as built; Prenzler 2022 p.1; City brochure 2020 pp.8-9) = -0.62 m LAT, -1.38 AHD, -1.50 MSL (as-built reading). Keep -1.5 m MSL in the model.
- Palm Beach seabed: pre-reef City survey contours 1.5-13.5 m at 0.5 m (Mortensen Fig 6, concept site); concept toe depths 11.4 m (offshore, 560 m out), 6.6 m (nearshore). Datum test: Navionics chart vs Fig 6a read along y = 3050 m (17 labels): chart=LAT RMS 0.52 m; converted to AHD (+0.76) RMS 0.29 m; to MSL (+0.88) 0.26 m; fitted shift 335 m, RMS 0.20 m. Supports chart datum = LAT.
- Palm Beach buoys (Daniels 2022 Table 1, datum not stated): PBO3 11.75 m at (-28.107191, 153.473845), about 214 m seaward of the offshore toe; PBO2 11.29; PBO1 11.33; PBO4 23.80 m. Natural reef 10-16 m (seaward), 5-9 m (shore edge).
- Narrowneck crest history (m AHD unless stated): Waikato -1.0; "initially designed" -0.67 (Black and Mead 2001, quoted); adopted 1999 -1.5 (-0.5 LAT); 2001 top-up -1.0 LAT; target since about 2004-07 -1.5 LAT = RL -2.5 AHD; before 2018 renewal low-tide depth over reef 2.5-2.6 m (Nettle 2017); **measured after the 2018 renewal -2.2 AHD** (Vieira da Silva 2021 AM p.5) = -1.44 LAT (MSQ) = -2.32 MSL. Use -2.2 AHD for the renewed reef; "designed -0.67, adopted -1.5" is history only; RWR (2019) "1.5 m below low tide" conflates levels, do not use.
- Narrowneck design depths (Jackson 2012 Fig 2): crest top -2.50, side contours -3.00 ... -5.0, -6.0, -8.00 outer limit; seabed around the reef from ten 2018-2020 surveys: inner edge -4/-5, body -6..-8, seaward -9/-10 (by eye, +-0.5-1 m).
- Narrowneck planform: 1998-99 split-V, 408 containers 20 m long, 3-4.5 m diameter, 60,000 m3; 2004 weir (2 containers) and flared wings, about 450 containers by 2007; 2017-Jun 2018 renewal, 84 added on the existing footprint, AMENDED shape with "minor changes" (shift up to about 20 m seaward, unverified); envelope 200-350 m alongshore x 400-500 m cross-shore.
- Palm Beach unexplained register values HEIGHT_M 5 / VOLUME 33,000 vs City text 60,000 t rock (1.82 t/m3 if 33,000 m3).
