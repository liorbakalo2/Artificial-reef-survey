# Image index — Cable Station Reef (Cables Reef), Leighton/Cottesloe, Perth, WA

Web image candidates for `cables-reef-wa`. None downloaded into the project — per project rules, images live only as indexed URLs (see `cables-reef-wa.json`). All direct URLs were checked with `curl -sI -A "Mozilla/5.0"` and returned `200 image/jpeg` (verified 2026-09-24) except the Google Maps entry, which is a live map link rather than a static image file.

## Images

1. **Cables-Reef.jpg** — Raised Water Research, "Cables Reef" spot page. Main photo on the page; likely the best single representative image of the reef/wave. https://raisedwaterresearch.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Cables-Reef.jpg
2. **cabletopturn.jpg** — Raised Water Research, same page. Surfer doing a top turn on a Cables Reef wave. https://raisedwaterresearch.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/cabletopturn.jpg
3. **Cables-Side-View.jpg** — Raised Water Research, same page, captioned "Side view of Cables Reef." Engineering cross-section diagram of the reef structure. https://raisedwaterresearch.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Cables-Side-View.jpg
4. **1948 Don Bancroft photo** — via Surfing Down South. Historical (pre-artificial-reef) water photo of surfers at the natural Cable Station reef break, 1948. https://surfingdownsouth.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/1948-Surfing-Cable-Station-Cottesloe.-water-pic-by-Don-Bancroft-Cropped.jpg
5. **1957 City Beach crew photo** — via Surfing Down South, captioned "1957 Brian Cole and his City Beach surfing mates heading to Cable Station reef." https://i0.wp.com/surfingdownsouth.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/1957-City-Beach-BC-crew-Morris-10-heading-to-Cable-Stn-BC-pic.jpg
6. **Google Maps satellite** — centred just offshore of the old Cable Station / McCall Centre building (32.01572°S 115.75262°E, the WA Heritage Council's surveyed coordinate for the onshore building; the reef itself is ~275 m offshore and submerged, so treat this as an approximate reference, not a surveyed structure position). https://www.google.com/maps/@-32.0157,115.7497,17z/data=!3m1!1e3

## Notes / what was rejected or not found

- **Wikimedia Commons**: searched (site category browsing + MediaSearch for "Cottesloe artificial reef") — no Commons file found that depicts the Cables Reef structure itself. Commons only has general Cottesloe/Leighton/Old Cable Station *building* photos, which were not used here because they don't show the reef.
- **ResearchGate figure** ("Location of the Cables Artificial Surfing Reef together with wave…", fig2 of Pattiaratchi et al. 2003) — exists and is directly on-topic (a location/wave-data diagram), but the page returned HTTP 403 to WebFetch and no direct image URL could be confirmed; not included as a checked entry.
- **Coastal Management (ICM) "artificial-surf-reefs" page** — checked for images; it discusses Cables Reef in text but its photos are all of Narrowneck/Palm Beach reefs, none of Cables.
- **News photos** (ABC, The West Australian, PerthNow) — general web search did not surface a specific dated news photo of the reef structure; not included rather than guessing.
- Two of the six entries (the 1948/1957 historical photos) depict the natural pre-1999 reef/surf break at the same site, not the constructed 1999 artificial reef itself — flagged here so this distinction isn't lost downstream.
