# Web images — Mount Maunganui Beach Reef ("Mount Reef")

All entries are link-only per project rules (nothing downloaded into the project folder). Full metadata in `mount-maunganui-reef.json`.

No Wikimedia Commons photo of this specific reef structure was found (checked "Mount Maunganui artificial reef site:commons.wikimedia.org" [R-search]; Commons has general Mount Maunganui beach/town photos but none identifiably showing the offshore geotextile reef). All five photo/survey images below instead come from **Raised Water Research**'s dedicated case-study page for this reef, a designer/researcher archive that names the reef in every filename and alt text and is the single best-documented image source found. All URLs were checked with `curl -sI -A "Mozilla/5.0" --http1.1` and returned `200 OK` / `Content-Type: image/jpeg` on 2026-09-24.

1. **Aerial view** — `Mount-Reef-Arial-1024x575.jpg` — shows the reef's offshore footprint and breaking surf from above.
2. **Installed structure** — `Mount-Reef-Installed.jpg` — the completed geotextile-container structure, credited to ASR (the reef's designer).
3. **Wave in action** — `Mount-Reef-Right-2-Mead-Borrero.jpg` — surfer riding a right-hander off the reef at sunset (credited Mead, Borrero).
4. **2007 multibeam survey** — `Mount-Reef-Multibean-2007.jpg` — bathymetric survey image of the submerged reef, sourced to Scarf 2009.
5. **2013 deflated bag** — `Mount-Reef-Deflated-Bag.jpg` — close-up of a damaged/deflated geotextile container at the reef apex, sourced to Dahm and Gibberd 2013; directly documents the deterioration described in the reef dossier.
6. **Map** — Google Maps satellite view centred near the Tay St / Marine Parade corner (-37.6471741, 176.1986412), the nearest documented fixed onshore reference point. The reef itself lay ~250 m offshore from here; no source gives the structure's own coordinates, and the reef was largely removed in 2014, so nothing structure-specific will be visible in current satellite imagery.
