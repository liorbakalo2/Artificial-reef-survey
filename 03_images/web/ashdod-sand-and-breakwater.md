## Rejected / not used

- surf-forecast.com break photos (Hakshtot, Ashdod-Hshover) were considered per the dossier's leads but not pulled into this index — they are wave/surf-condition shots of the breaks, not of the port/breakwater structure itself, and surf-forecast.com does not publish a clear reusable license.
- ashdod10.co.il / ashdodnet.com / ashdodi.com article pages (2021–2023 floating-breakwater coverage) were not converted into image entries this pass — their embedded photos could not be resolved to stable direct image URLs during this session's tooling; a human pass on those pages would likely add protest/council-chamber photos.
- he.wikipedia.org articles (נמל אשדוד, רצועת חוף אשדוד) were used as text sources already in the dossier; their embedded images largely duplicate the Commons files already indexed above.
