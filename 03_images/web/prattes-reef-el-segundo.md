## Notes

- Searched Wikimedia Commons directly (`site:commons.wikimedia.org`) for "Pratte's Reef" / "Chevron Reef" — found nothing depicting the reef itself, only unrelated El Segundo/Chevron-refinery-land files (e.g. `Chevron_El_Segundo_refinery,_2007.jpg`, an onshore refinery photo, not the offshore reef). Consistent with the researcher's own dossier note (image leads section) that a Commons search returned zero hits. No Commons entry exists for this reef as of 2026-09-24.
- Surfline's 2008 feature ("SANDBAGGED," Evan Fontaine, Oct 11 2008, https://www.surfline.com/templates/article_html.cfm?n=2&id=19261&p=1) has a 13-photo gallery credited to photographer Jeremiah Klein — copyrighted news photography, explicitly excluded here per the dossier's own caution; link to the article rather than hotlink its images.
- All six photo/diagram URLs above were verified live with `curl -sI -A "Mozilla/5.0"` returning HTTP 200 and an `image/*` content-type (checked 2026-09-24).
- No dated/named photographer credit was available for the Raised Water Research or Coastal Frontiers images beyond the organization itself; treat "credit" as the hosting organization, not an individual photographer.
